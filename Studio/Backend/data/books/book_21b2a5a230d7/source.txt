Two People, One City

Mina is at a bus stop. A young man looks at the street numbers.

"Excuse me," he says. "Is this Pine Street?"

"Yes, it is," Mina answers.

"Thank you. I am Leo. I am new in this city."

"I am Mina. What is your home country?"

"Italy. My new address is twenty-four Pine Street."

Mina laughs. "Really? I live at number twenty, near your building. We are neighbours."

Leo is twenty years old. Mina is nineteen. They both study at City College. Leo studies art, and Mina studies English.

Their bus is number eight, but it is late. They talk while they wait. Mina gives Leo her phone number. He reads the digits back to her, and Mina says they are correct.

When the bus arrives, they sit together. Leo came to the stop as a stranger. Ten minutes later, he has a new neighbour and a new friend.
