A Very Busy Morning

Leo has a doctor's appointment at eleven. Before he leaves, he has five jobs to do.

He writes a list:

First, wash the dishes. Second, call Grandma. Third, buy milk. Fourth, send an email. Fifth, take the dog outside.

At eight thirty, Leo eats breakfast. First, he washes the dishes. Next, he calls Grandma, but she talks for twenty minutes.

At nine forty, Leo walks the dog. After that, he goes to the shop. There is a long line, and he starts to hurry.

He arrives home at ten thirty. He sends the email, puts the milk in the fridge, and checks his list.

At ten forty, Leo is ready and leaves for the appointment. The bus comes at ten forty-five. He arrives at the doctor's office at ten fifty-eight.

He does not take a break, but he finishes the list and is not late. His morning is busy, but every job is complete.
