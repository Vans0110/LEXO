# Chapter 3: Home & Furniture

## Цель главы

Описать квартиру, комнаты, мебель, технику и положение предметов.

## Ядро

- Слова: home, apartment, room, kitchen, bedroom, bathroom, living room, bed,
  table, chair, sofa, wardrobe, shelf, lamp, fridge, cooker, kettle, cup,
  plate, towel, key.
- Грамматика: `there is/are`, `this/that`, `in/on/under/next to`.

## Book 1: New Apartment

Target: apartment, floor, living room, bedroom, sofa, wardrobe, shelf, lamp.
Recycle: address, number, city.

Oliver moves into Apartment eight on the third floor of a building in the city. The apartment is small, but it has everything he needs.

There is a green sofa in the living room. A low table is in front of it. There are two chairs near the window and a lamp next to the sofa.

In the bedroom, there is a bed and a large wardrobe. Oliver puts his clothes in the wardrobe and his books on a shelf.

His friend Mia comes to help. She carries a plant into the living room.

In the kitchen box, she finds a cup, plate, glass, spoon, fork, knife, and kettle. Oliver puts them beside the fridge and keeps his key on the shelf.

"Where do you want this?" she asks.

"Put it on the table by the window, please."

They sit on the floor because the chairs are still in boxes. Oliver looks at the sofa, the lamp, and the books.

"It does not look like a box anymore," Mia says.

Oliver smiles. It looks like a home.

## Book 2: The Room with Two Doors

Target: bathroom, door, mirror, towel, shower, left, right.
Recycle: room, bedroom, kitchen, next to.

Maya visits her cousin's new home. After a long bus ride, she wants to wash her hands.

"Where is the bathroom?" she asks.

"Go down the hall," her cousin says. "There are two doors. The bathroom is on the left."

Maya opens the left door. There is a bed, a wardrobe, and a small desk.

"This is the bedroom," she calls.

"Sorry! The bathroom is on the right."

Maya opens the other door. There is a mirror above the sink, a towel next to the shower, and soap on a small shelf.

Later, her cousin puts a blue sign on the bathroom door. It has no words, only a picture of a shower.

"Now guests can find the right room," he says.

Maya laughs. "Or you can remember which side is left."

## Book 3: A Plant by the Window

Target: window, plant, light, corner, between, above, below.
Recycle: table, shelf, sofa, lamp.

Nina gives her neighbours a large green plant. They want to find a good place for it.

"There is space next to the sofa," Tom says.

"But there is not much light in that corner," Ava answers.

They try the kitchen table, but the plant is between two chairs and people cannot sit there.

They put it on a shelf above the television. The shelf is too small.

Finally, Ava sees an empty place below the living-room window. There is light in the morning, and the plant is not near the cooker or the door.

They put a small table by the window and place the plant on it.

The next day, their cat sits under the table and looks up at the leaves.

"The cat likes this place too," Tom says.

Now the empty corner feels warm and alive.

## Book 4: The Quiet Flat Upstairs

Target: building, upstairs, downstairs, entrance, lift, stairs, neighbour.
Recycle: address, floor, apartment, room.

Amir lives in Flat five on the second floor. One evening, he hears a soft sound upstairs.

The apartment above him is number eight. Amir thinks it is empty, but there is light under the door.

He takes the lift to the third floor and knocks. An older woman opens the door.

"Hello," she says. "I am Mrs Park, your new neighbour."

Boxes stand in the entrance. A chair is in the hall, and books are on the stairs.

Amir helps her carry two boxes into the living room. Mrs Park shows him a small piano.

"Was that the sound?" Amir asks.

"Yes. I play quietly in the evening."

The music is not a problem. It is soft and beautiful. Downstairs, Amir can hear it through the ceiling. The flat upstairs is no longer empty, and the building has a new neighbour.

## Book 5: Where Is My Cup?

Target: cup, plate, glass, spoon, fork, knife, kettle, fridge.
Recycle: kitchen, bedroom, bathroom, on, under, next to.

Ella makes tea, but her favourite yellow cup is not in the kitchen cupboard.

There are white cups next to the plates. There are glasses above the fridge. The spoons, forks, and knives are in a drawer. The yellow cup is not there.

Ella looks in the living room. It is not on the table or under the sofa. Her house key is on the table, but the cup is not.

She looks in the bedroom. It is not next to the lamp.

In the bathroom mirror, she sees a towel, soap, and a small plant, but no cup.

At last, the kettle makes a sound. Ella turns back to the kitchen. Her yellow cup is behind the kettle, next to the cooker.

Her brother put it there after breakfast.

"Please put cups in the cupboard," Ella tells him.

He smiles. "I can do that. But first, can I have some tea?"
