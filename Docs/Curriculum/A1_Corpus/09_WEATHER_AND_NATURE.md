# Chapter 9: Weather & Nature

## Цель главы

Описать погоду, сезон, природное место и происходящее сейчас.

## Ядро

- Слова: sun, cloud, rain, snow, wind, storm, hot, cold, warm, cool, dry, wet,
  sky, tree, flower, river, lake, forest, hill, season.
- Данные: температура, прогноз, месяцы и времена года.
- Грамматика: прилагательные, базовый Present Continuous.

## Book 1: A Rainy Day

Target: rain, cloud, thunder, wet, storm, fresh, hide.
Recycle: dog, home, window, walk.

Dark clouds cover the sky, and heavy rain is falling. Julia planned to meet a friend in the park, but she stays home.

At noon, thunder makes a loud sound. Wind moves the wet trees, and Julia's small dog, Pip, tries to hide under the table.

"It is only a storm," Julia says. She sits on the floor beside him.

For an hour, rain hits the windows. The street becomes wet, and water runs beside the road.

At five, the storm moves away. The clouds open, and the evening sky turns orange.

Julia puts on boots and takes Pip outside. Drops of water are shining on the trees and flowers. The air feels cool and fresh.

Pip jumps over a small pool of water, then runs through a larger one. Now his feet are wet.

Julia laughs. A rainy day changes their plan, but it does not ruin the whole day.

## Book 2: Snow in the Morning

Target: snow, ice, freeze, melt, soft, deep, careful.
Recycle: cold, boots, coat, morning.

When Noah wakes up, the street is white. Snow is falling, and it looks soft.

The temperature is five degrees below zero. Water can freeze, and some water on the road has turned to ice.

Noah wears a coat, wool hat, gloves, and boots. Outside, the snow is deep near the garden wall.

His neighbour Mrs Chen is careful because she has two shopping bags. Noah carries one bag and helps her across the icy path.

At school, children make a small snowman. By afternoon, the sun appears and the temperature rises to two degrees above zero.

Snow on the roof begins to melt. Water drops onto the path.

Noah's snowman becomes smaller, but it is still there when he goes home.

"Winter made it this morning," he tells Mrs Chen.

"And the warmer weather will take it away," she says.

## Book 3: The Hottest Afternoon

Target: hot, shade, temperature, dry, thirsty, bottle, rest.
Recycle: water, park, tree, summer.

It is July, in the summer season, and the temperature is thirty-six degrees. The sun is bright while Maya and Ben walk through the park.

The grass is dry, and there are no clouds in the sky. Ben has a hat, but Maya does not.

"I am very hot and thirsty," Maya says.

They sit in the shade under a large tree. Ben gives Maya some water from his bottle.

A sign says the small cafe is three hundred metres away. They walk slowly and stop again near a fountain.

At the cafe, Maya buys a cold drink and a light hat. They decide not to play tennis.

Instead, they rest inside until five o'clock. The temperature falls to thirty-one degrees.

On the way home, a cool wind begins.

"Next time, we check the weather before we leave," Ben says.

Maya touches her new hat. "And I bring my own water."

## Book 4: A Day in the Forest

Target: forest, path, hill, river, leaf, bird, quiet, nature.
Recycle: map, walk, weather, lunch.

Four friends spend a spring day in a forest. The weather is warm but not hot, and the path is dry.

They follow a green path up a small hill. New leaves are growing on the trees, and birds are singing above them.

At the top, they can see a river and a lake below. The map shows a picnic place beside the water.

The friends walk down carefully. Near the river, they eat lunch and listen to the sounds of nature.

Leo wants to take a different path home, but there is no sign.

"We should stay on the green path," Ana says.

They return the same way. On the hill, light rain and thunder begin like a short storm. The wet leaves keep some water off them, and the rain stops after ten minutes.

The forest is quiet again. They imagine its orange leaves in autumn. The friends take only photographs and carry all their rubbish home.

## Book 5: Four Seasons at One Window

Target: spring, summer, autumn, winter, season, change, grow, fall.
Recycle: tree, flower, rain, snow, warm, cold.

Ella takes one photograph from her kitchen window every season. A forest and small hill are visible behind the tree.

In March, spring begins. Small green leaves grow on the tree, and yellow flowers open below it. A river and lake shine behind the garden. Rain falls often.

In July, summer makes the tree full and green. The days are long and warm. Children play outside in the evening.

In October, autumn colours the leaves orange and red. Wind moves them across the street.

In January, winter covers the branches with snow. The days are short, and the window is cold.

Ella puts the four photographs together. The seasons change the tree, so it looks like four different trees.

Her young nephew points to the autumn picture. "Is that today?"

"No. Today is spring," Ella says. "But every season comes back."

They look outside. New leaves are growing again.
