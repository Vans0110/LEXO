The Dream Job Wall

A teacher asks every student to add one future dream job to a classroom wall.

Mina wants to become a nurse because she likes helping people. Joel wants to design buildings. Sara would like to teach music. Ben is not sure.

"Can I write more than one job?" he asks.

"Of course. A dream can change," the teacher says.

Ben draws a camera, a computer, and a train. He likes taking photographs, using technology, and travelling.

Under the pictures, he writes: `I want a job with stories and places.`

His goal is to learn how good design can help people.

The teacher suggests journalism. Ben does not know much about it, so he plans to visit the school newspaper.

The wall does not tell students exactly which profession they will choose. Someday their dream job may change, but they can improve and create new skills.

Ben leaves space under his pictures for a new idea.
