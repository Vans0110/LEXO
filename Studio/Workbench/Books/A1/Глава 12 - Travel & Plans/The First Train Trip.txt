The First Train Trip

Nina and her brother are going to travel to their aunt by train. It is Nina's first long journey and train trip.

Their tickets say:

`June eighteenth — depart nine twenty — Platform four — Carriage three — Seats twenty-one and twenty-two.`

They arrive at the station at eight fifty. Nina checks the platform screen twice.

"We have thirty minutes," her brother says.

They buy water and carry one suitcase and two backpacks to Platform four.

At nine ten, the train arrives. They find Carriage three, put the suitcase above their seats, and sit by the window.

The train departs on time. Every guest has a seat. It is going to arrive at twelve forty.

Nina sends her aunt a message with the arrival time.

When the train starts moving, Nina smiles. The ticket looked complicated at home, but every number has helped them find the right place.
