# Chapter 2: Family & People

## Цель главы

Назвать родственников, возраст, внешность и основные черты характера.

## Ядро

- Слова: mother, father, parent, sister, brother, grandmother, grandfather,
  daughter, son, husband, wife, young, old, tall, short, kind, funny, quiet.
- Фразы: `I have...`, `She has...`, `This is my...`, `He looks...`
- Грамматика: `have/has`, притяжательные формы, описательные прилагательные.

## Book 1: Grandma's Dinner

Target: grandmother, grandfather, parents, brother, family, kind, funny.
Recycle: name, age, live.

Emma has a big family. Every Sunday, she visits her grandparents with her parents and her younger brother, Sam.

Her grandmother is seventy-two. She is short, kind, and very active. Her grandfather is seventy-five. He has grey hair and glasses. He is quiet at first, but he has many funny stories.

Today the family has dinner together. Emma's mother puts plates on the table. Her father brings water. Sam carries the bread, but he eats one piece before dinner.

"Sam!" Emma says.

Grandfather laughs. "He is like his father at that age."

After dinner, Grandmother shows Emma an old family photograph. Emma sees her mother as a young girl.

"You have the same smile," Grandmother says.

Emma looks around the table. The people are different ages and have different faces, but they all laugh at the same stories.

## Book 2: The Family Photo

Target: aunt, uncle, cousin, husband, wife, daughter, son.
Recycle: grandmother, grandfather, mother, father, young, old.

Noah finds a large photograph in a box. There are nine people in it, but he does not know all their names.

He takes the photo to his mother.

"Who is this tall man?" Noah asks.

"That is your Uncle Paul. He is my brother."

"And the woman next to him?"

"She is his wife, Aunt Rosa. Uncle Paul is her husband. Their daughter is your cousin Lily."

Noah points to a small boy. "Is this Dad?"

His mother smiles. "Yes. Your father is eight in this picture. He is Grandma and Grandpa's son."

At the centre are Noah's grandparents. His grandmother has dark hair in the photo, and his grandfather has a black hat.

Noah writes each name on a small piece of paper and puts it under the right person. Now the old photograph is not a group of strangers. It is a map of his family.

## Book 3: My Very Tall Brother

Target: tall, short, hair, eyes, beard, glasses, wear.
Recycle: brother, sister, age, clothes, colour.

Lena waits for her brother at a busy train station. Her friend Eva is with her.

"What does your brother look like?" Eva asks.

"Max is very tall," Lena says. "He has short brown hair and green eyes."

"Does he have a beard?"

"No beard, but he wears black glasses. He is twenty-four."

Many people leave the train. Eva sees a tall man with glasses.

"Is that Max?"

"No. That man has a blue coat. Max has a red jacket."

Then a short man in a red jacket waves at Lena.

Eva looks surprised. "I thought your brother was very tall."

Lena laughs. "He is tall in my family. I am only one metre fifty-five."

The short man is not Max. He is waving to someone behind them. At last, Max appears. He is tall, has black glasses, and carries a red jacket over his arm.

## Book 4: A Birthday for Dad

Target: birthday, date, year, candle, present, remember, surprise.
Recycle: father, mother, brother, sister, age, number.

The date is May fourteenth, and Oliver's father is forty-nine today. The family wants to give him a surprise after work.

Oliver buys a small cake. His sister Mia buys five large candles and four small candles. The large candles mean ten years each.

"Five large candles are fifty," Oliver says. "Dad is forty-nine."

Mia removes one small candle. "Now it is forty-nine."

Their kind aunt, funny uncle, and young cousin arrive early. The cousin is their aunt and uncle's daughter. Dad's wife makes her husband's favourite dinner. Their younger son hides the present under the table. He almost tells Dad about it on the phone, but Mia stops him.

At seven, Dad opens the door. The room is dark.

"Is anyone home?" he asks.

The lights come on. "Happy birthday!"

Dad looks at the cake, the candles, and his family. He remembers every family birthday, but today everyone remembers his.

## Book 5: Who Has the Keys?

Target: whose, mine, yours, his, hers, careful, pocket.
Recycle: family members, key, room, table, chair.

The Green family is ready to leave the house, but the car keys are not on the hall table.

"Whose keys are these?" Ben asks. "And whose small silver key is this?"

"That one is mine," Grandma says. "It is for my apartment."

Mother looks in her bag. She is Father's wife. Father checks his coat pockets. Their daughter, Ben's quiet sister, looks under the sofa.

"Dad often puts the car keys near his glasses," she says.

His glasses are on the kitchen table, but the keys are not there.

Then the family dog walks into the room. Its funny eyes look at Ben, and something makes a small sound on its collar.

"The keys!" Ben says.

The car keys are on the dog's collar. Father put them there when both hands were full.

"Those keys are yours," Mother tells him, "so please be more careful."

The parents and children leave ten minutes late. This time, Father keeps the keys in his own pocket.
