# Chapter 14: Technology & Communication

## Цель главы

Использовать базовые устройства, сообщения и звонки, описать простую
техническую проблему.

## Ядро

- Слова: phone, computer, laptop, screen, keyboard, button, battery, charger,
  headphones, message, call, email, internet, online, send, receive, connect.
- Грамматика: повтор настоящего; ограниченные простые прошлые события.

## Book 1: The Message with No Name

Target: message, send, receive, contact, group, unknown, reply.
Recycle: phone, date, time, place.

Mia receives a message and an email attachment on Tuesday:

`Party at Green Cafe, Friday at seven. Please come!`

The number is not in her contacts. There is no name.

Mia replies, `Hello. Who is this?`

No answer comes.

She sends the message to her friends' group. Ben knows the cafe, but he did not send it. Rosa has a party on Friday, but it starts at eight.

Then Leo checks the number.

"The last digit is wrong," he says. "This is almost my sister's number."

Mia changes the final six to an eight, sends an email, and calls.

Leo's sister answers. She sent the invitation, but she typed one digit incorrectly.

Mia deletes the unknown number and sends the invitation to the right person.

One small digit sent a party message to a stranger. Careful checking sends it home.

## Book 2: A Phone with No Power

Target: battery, charger, power, plug, empty, full, charge.
Recycle: phone, call, bus, time.

Jon is travelling home when his phone battery falls to one percent. He needs to call his sister at the station.

The screen turns black before he can send a message.

Jon has a charger and offline map in his backpack, but there is no plug on the bus. At the station, he finds a charging point near the ticket office.

He connects the phone. After five minutes, the battery shows three percent. That is enough for a short call.

"I am at the main entrance," Jon tells his sister.

"I am waiting at the north entrance."

Jon keeps the phone connected for ten more minutes, then walks across the station. His sister is there.

At home, Jon charges the battery until it is full. He also puts a small power bank in his bag.

The next journey will not depend on one empty battery.

## Book 3: Grandpa Goes Online

Target: online, video call, camera, microphone, click, connect.
Recycle: computer, family, speak, see.

Grandpa Luis wants to see his granddaughter in another country. Ana helps him go online and make his first video call.

She turns on the power, opens the laptop, and connects it to the internet. Grandpa uses the keyboard while Ana puts on headphones.

"Click this blue button," she says.

Grandpa clicks the red button instead, and the program closes.

They open it again. This time, Ana shows him the camera and microphone buttons.

His granddaughter calls. Grandpa can hear her, but she cannot see him.

"Your camera is off," Ana says.

He clicks the camera button. His face appears, but only the top of his head is on the screen. Ana moves the computer.

At last, Grandpa and his granddaughter can see and hear each other.

They talk for twenty minutes. After the call, Grandpa writes three steps on a card. Next Sunday, he plans to connect without Ana's help.

## Book 4: The Wrong Email

Target: email, subject, attachment, address, delete, forward, check.
Recycle: name, work, send, file.

Eva writes an email to her manager. She adds a work schedule as an attachment.

In the address box, she types `Martin Green`, but her manager is `Martha Green`. The computer chooses Martin's address.

Eva clicks send. One second later, she sees the mistake.

"Oh no."

She sends Martin another email: `Please delete my last message. It was for Martha.`

Martin replies, `No problem. I did not forward it. I deleted it.`

Eva writes the email again. This time, she checks the full address, the subject, and the attachment before sending.

Martha receives the correct file and thanks her.

Eva changes the contact names in her computer to `Martha — Manager` and `Martin — Market Office`.

Technology can send a message very quickly. It can also send a mistake very quickly, so Eva now checks before every click.

## Book 5: No Internet Evening

Target: internet, connection, offline, download, screen, device, solve.
Recycle: music, game, friend, evening.

Four friends meet at Ben's home to watch a film online. Ben's laptop battery is full, and it has a keyboard, headphones, charger, camera, and microphone. At seven, the internet connection stops.

They restart the computer and press the router button. Nothing changes. Every device is offline. Ben plugs in the charger, but it does not solve the connection problem.

"What can we do?" Maya asks.

Ben has downloaded music, but not the film. Rosa finds a board game in a cupboard. Leo brings paper and pencils.

They play the game, draw funny pictures, and listen to music from the laptop. Nobody looks at a phone screen.

At nine, the internet returns.

"The film is ready now," Ben says.

The others are in the middle of the game. They decide to finish it.

At ten, they are too tired for a two-hour film.

They plan to watch it next Friday. The connection problem did not end their evening. It changed it into something they did not need to download.
