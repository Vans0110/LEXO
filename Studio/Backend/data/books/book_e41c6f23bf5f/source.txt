The Library Table

Four university students have a science project for their course. They meet at a library table after school at four o'clock.

Jamal brings a laptop computer and a backpack. Rosa brings two books. Ken has a folder with notebook pages, but Lily has only one pen and one pencil.

"Do we have everything?" Jamal asks.

"We do not have a ruler," Ken says.

Lily borrows one from the library desk. A guard shows her where to sign.

The students read a question in their project book. They do not know the answer, so Rosa finds it in a dictionary. Jamal writes it on the laptop.

At six, they study and practise their short presentation. Each student speaks for one minute, as in an exam.

"We work well together," Lily says.

They put their books, notes, ruler, and pens back into their bags. The project is not finished, but everyone knows what to do before the next meeting.
