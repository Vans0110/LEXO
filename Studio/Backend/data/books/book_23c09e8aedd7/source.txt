A Rainy Day

Dark clouds cover the sky, and heavy rain is falling. Julia planned to meet a friend in the park, but she stays home.

At noon, thunder makes a loud sound. Wind moves the wet trees, and Julia's small dog, Pip, tries to hide under the table.

"It is only a storm," Julia says. She sits on the floor beside him.

For an hour, rain hits the windows. The street becomes wet, and water runs beside the road.

At five, the storm moves away. The clouds open, and the evening sky turns orange.

Julia puts on boots and takes Pip outside. Drops of water are shining on the trees and flowers. The air feels cool and fresh.

Pip jumps over a small pool of water, then runs through a larger one. Now his feet are wet.

Julia laughs. A rainy day changes their plan, but it does not ruin the whole day.
