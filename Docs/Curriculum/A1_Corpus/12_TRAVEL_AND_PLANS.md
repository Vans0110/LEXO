# Chapter 12: Travel & Plans

## Цель главы

Подготовить простую поездку, понять билет и бронирование, рассказать о планах.

## Ядро

- Слова: travel, trip, journey, train, plane, airport, hotel, passport,
  ticket, booking, luggage, suitcase, arrive, depart, stay, visit.
- Данные: дата, время, платформа, вагон, место, номер комнаты, вес багажа.
- Грамматика: `going to`, повтор будущих планов.

## Book 1: The First Train Trip

Target: trip, depart, arrive, platform, carriage, seat, ticket.
Recycle: station, time, number, bag.

Nina and her brother are going to travel to their aunt by train. It is Nina's first long journey and train trip.

Their tickets say:

`June eighteenth — depart nine twenty — Platform four — Carriage three — Seats twenty-one and twenty-two.`

They arrive at the station at eight fifty. Nina checks the platform screen twice.

"We have thirty minutes," her brother says.

They buy water and carry one suitcase and two backpacks to Platform four.

At nine ten, the train arrives. They find Carriage three, put the suitcase above their seats, and sit by the window.

The train departs on time. Every guest has a seat. It is going to arrive at twelve forty.

Nina sends her aunt a message with the arrival time.

When the train starts moving, Nina smiles. The ticket looked complicated at home, but every number has helped them find the right place.

## Book 2: A Room with a View

Target: hotel, booking, reception, guest, room, key card, breakfast.
Recycle: name, passport, number, floor.

After a long travel day, Daniel arrives from the airport with his luggage at the Sea View Hotel at three. The receptionist welcomes him. At reception, he gives his first name, last name, and passport.

"I have a booking for two nights," he says.

The receptionist finds the guest's booking on the computer.

"Room five hundred and seven, on the fifth floor. Breakfast is from seven to ten."

After check-in, she gives Daniel a key card and a small hotel map. His room has a bed, desk, bathroom mirror, and window.

Daniel opens the curtains. He can see the sea, but also a large building crane.

At first, he laughs. Then the crane moves, and the sea appears again.

Daniel sends a photograph to his family: `The room really has a view — most of the time.` He will stay for two nights.

The next morning, he goes downstairs at eight. He remembers the breakfast time because it is written beside his room number.

## Book 3: The Bag at the Airport

Target: airport, luggage, suitcase, tag, weight, check-in, similar.
Recycle: colour, size, name, flight.

Maya is flying to Rome. At check-in, the weight of her blue suitcase is eighteen kilos. The worker puts a tag with Maya's name and flight number on it.

The worker weighs it again before it moves to the plane. Maya keeps her passport and a small red scarf in her hand luggage.

After the flight, Maya waits beside the luggage belt. Her passport is in her hand. A similar blue suitcase appears. It is the same size as hers.

She takes it, but the tag says `Martin Cole`.

"This is not my bag," she says.

A man nearby is holding another blue suitcase. He checks its tag.

"And this is not mine."

They exchange the bags. Maya opens a small side pocket and finds her red scarf. Now she is sure.

Before leaving the airport, she ties the scarf around the handle.

On her return flight, many suitcases are blue, black, and grey. Maya can see her own bag immediately. A small bright detail prevents another mistake.

## Book 4: Our Summer Plan

Target: plan, holiday, choose, distance, cost, beach, mountain.
Recycle: calendar, travel, hotel, weather.

The Reed family is planning a five-day holiday and journey in August. They want to visit a new place.

Mum wants to visit the beach. Dad prefers the mountains. Their daughter wants a hotel with a swimming pool.

They compare two places. The distance to Blue Bay is two hundred kilometres. The hotel costs one hundred and twenty dollars a night. Hill Town is ninety kilometres away, and a small apartment costs seventy dollars a night.

The weather at Blue Bay is usually hot. Hill Town is cooler and has forest walks.

The family has a budget of five hundred dollars. Five hotel nights at Blue Bay are too expensive. A train carriage is cheaper than plane tickets.

They choose Hill Town for four nights and will stay in the apartment. Each person gets a seat on the train. The apartment has a pool nearby, and there is a lake for swimming.

Their holiday is not exactly Mum's beach plan or Dad's mountain plan. It is a new plan with something for everyone.

## Book 5: One Day across the City

Target: plan, route, booking, ready, remember, finally.
Recycle: directions, food, money, weather, time.

Four friends plan to spend a holiday Saturday across the city and visit four stops: a museum, a market, a park, and a small hotel restaurant.

They meet at nine at Metro Station three. Leo brings the route map. It shows a direct line, the previous stop, two corners, and where to turn left. They get on a south train and pay the fare. Ana has the museum booking. Kim carries water and jackets because rain is possible. Omar has eighty dollars in cash for the group lunch.

At the museum, they show the booking number. At the market, they buy fruit and check the change. In the park, light rain begins, so they put on their jackets.

At six, traffic is heavy. They get off the metro, cross a road, and walk straight to the restaurant, but they turn down the wrong street. Ana asks a shop worker for directions. The restaurant is opposite a bank.

Finally, they arrive at hotel reception on time. Before they depart, they give the restaurant booking number to the receptionist.

At dinner, they look at the route on Leo's map. Four stops filled the whole day, but nobody wants to remove one. They remember each wrong turn and feel ready to plan another trip.
