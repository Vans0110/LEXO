A Plant by the Window

Nina gives her neighbours a large green plant. They want to find a good place for it.

"There is space next to the sofa," Tom says.

"But there is not much light in that corner," Ava answers.

They try the kitchen table, but the plant is between two chairs and people cannot sit there.

They put it on a shelf above the television. The shelf is too small.

Finally, Ava sees an empty place below the living-room window. There is light in the morning, and the plant is not near the cooker or the door.

They put a small table by the window and place the plant on it.

The next day, their cat sits under the table and looks up at the leaves.

"The cat likes this place too," Tom says.

Now the empty corner feels warm and alive.
