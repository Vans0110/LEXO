# Chapter 7: Clothes & Shopping

## Цель главы

Назвать одежду, цвет, размер и материал, спросить цену и сделать покупку.

## Ядро

- Слова: shirt, T-shirt, trousers, jeans, dress, skirt, jacket, coat, shoes,
  boots, hat, size, try on, fit, wear, cotton, wool, leather.
- Данные: размеры, цены, скидки в простом виде.
- Грамматика: `this/these`, прилагательные, `too big/small`.

## Book 1: The Blue Jacket

Target: jacket, try on, size, fit, warm, comfortable, price, cash.
Recycle: blue, winter, shop, buy.

Anna needs a new jacket for winter. Her old jacket is too small.

After work, she visits a clothing shop. There are black, green, red, and blue jackets near the door.

Anna tries on a black jacket in size small. It fits, but it is not very warm. Then she sees a blue jacket made of thick wool.

Her sweater, scarf, hat, and gloves fit under it. A longer coat feels loose, but this jacket is not tight.

"Can I try this on in size medium?" she asks.

The shop assistant brings it to her. The sleeves are the right length, and the jacket feels comfortable.

"How much is it?"

"The price is eighty dollars. Today there is a ten-dollar discount."

Anna has fifty dollars in cash, so she pays the rest by card.

Outside, light snow is falling. Anna puts on the new jacket immediately.

The blue colour is bright, the size is right, and the winter air does not feel so cold.

## Book 2: The Shoe Shop

Target: shoes, boots, pair, leather, foot, tight, loose, another.
Recycle: size, try on, price, black.

David needs shoes for a new office job. He passes T-shirts, trousers, jeans, and winter boots, then finds a pair of black leather shoes in a shop window.

"What size do you wear?" the assistant asks.

"Size forty-two."

David tries on the shoes. The right shoe feels good, but the left one is too tight.

"Can I try another pair?"

The assistant brings size forty-three. Now both shoes feel loose.

She looks at David's feet. "Your left foot is a little larger. Try this pair in size forty-two and a half."

David walks around the shop. The shoes fit well.

They cost ninety dollars. A cheaper pair costs sixty, but it is not leather and does not feel comfortable.

David buys the better pair. On his first day at work, he stands for many hours. His feet are tired, but the shoes do not hurt.

## Book 3: The Wrong Red Dress

Target: dress, skirt, label, changing room, same, different, return.
Recycle: red, size, bag, receipt.

Sofia buys a red dress for her sister's birthday dinner. In the changing room, size medium fits perfectly. A skirt and cotton shirt hang beside it.

At home, Sofia opens the shopping bag. The dress inside looks the same, but the label says size small. It is the same style as the medium dress.

The next morning, she returns to the shop with the dress and receipt.

"I think I have the wrong dress," she says.

The assistant checks the number on the receipt. Another customer bought the same red dress at the same time. The two bags were different colours, but someone put the dresses in the wrong bags.

The other customer has already called the shop. She has Sofia's medium dress.

That afternoon, both women return. They exchange the dresses and laugh about the mistake.

At the birthday dinner, Sofia's sister wears a red skirt. The sisters are not dressed the same, but they are very close.

## Book 4: Ready for Winter

Target: coat, scarf, gloves, hat, sweater, wool, temperature, below.
Recycle: boots, warm, cold, wear.

Omar is moving to a colder city. In his home town, the winter temperature is usually ten degrees above zero. In his new city, it can be fifteen degrees below zero.

His friend Lina helps him prepare.

"You need a warm coat, boots, gloves, a scarf, and a hat," she says.

Omar has a cotton sweater, but Lina shows him a wool sweater.

"Wool is warmer."

They check a weather report. Snow is coming on Friday. Omar buys a dark coat, waterproof boots, and two pairs of gloves.

On Friday morning, he wears a jacket under the coat and comfortable shoes with his boots. He puts on everything at once and feels too warm inside the bus.

"You can take off the hat and scarf on the bus," Lina says.

Outside, the wind is strong. Omar puts them on again.

Winter needs more clothes, but each item has a job.

## Book 5: A Gift for a Friend

Target: gift, choose, style, favourite, exchange, receipt, wrap.
Recycle: shirt, colour, size, price.

Maya wants to buy a birthday gift for her friend Ben. She knows his favourite colour is green, but she does not know his shirt size.

In a shop, Maya passes dresses, skirts, leather jackets, T-shirts, trousers, and jeans. Then she finds a green cotton shirt and takes it to the changing room.

"Can he exchange it if it does not fit?" she asks.

"Yes," the assistant says. "Keep the receipt."

Maya calls Ben's brother.

"What size does Ben wear?"

"Usually medium, but some shirts are small."

Maya chooses size large because the shirt has a narrow style. It costs thirty-five dollars. The assistant wraps it in blue paper.

At the party, Ben opens the gift and tries on the shirt. The colour is perfect, but the sleeves are too long.

The next day, Maya and Ben exchange it for a medium. Ben tries on the shirt and wears it to lunch.

"Now the gift fits," Maya says.

"And it is still my favourite colour."
