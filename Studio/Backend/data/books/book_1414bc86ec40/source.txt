Welcome to Maple Street

Nora moves into Apartment six at fifteen Maple Street. In the entrance hall, she finds a small welcome table.

Mr Lee, an older neighbour, sits behind the table.

"Welcome to Maple Street," he says. "We have a contact form for new neighbours."

The form asks for basic information. Nora writes her first name, last name, age, apartment number, phone number, and email address.

"Where are you from?" Mr Lee asks.

"I am from Ukraine. I am twenty years old, and I am a university student."

"What languages do you speak?"

"Ukrainian, English, and a little Polish."

Two neighbours come into the hall. Mr Lee introduces them to Nora. One is a nurse from Apartment four. The other is a cook from Apartment nine.

Later, Nora puts the welcome card on her table. It has useful phone numbers and the names of her neighbours. Her apartment is new, but the building already feels friendly.
