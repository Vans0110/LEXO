# Chapter 17: Animals & Pets

## Цель главы

Назвать домашних и знакомых животных, описать их и ежедневный уход.

## Ядро

- Слова: animal, pet, dog, cat, bird, fish, rabbit, horse, tail, wing, fur,
  feed, walk, clean, care, friendly, wild, owner.
- Грамматика: Present Simple, `have to`, описания.

## Book 1: The Dog Walking Plan

Target: dog, owner, walk, feed, bowl, lead, twice.
Recycle: morning, evening, schedule.

Maya wants a dog, but her parents say a pet needs daily care.

They make a one-week plan with their neighbour's dog, Bruno. He is active and friendly. His owner asks Maya to walk him twice a day, fill his water bowl, feed him at six, and use his lead.

On Monday morning, Maya wakes up early and uses Bruno's lead. In the evening, rain is falling, but Bruno still needs a walk.

On Wednesday, Maya wants to meet friends after school. She first goes home to feed Bruno.

During the walk, Bruno stops to look at a horse behind a fence.

By Sunday, she understands that a dog is not only games and soft fur. A dog has a schedule every day.

Her parents ask, "Do you still want a pet?"

"Yes," Maya says, "but not this month. I want to be ready first."

She continues walking Bruno once a week. Learning to care for an animal helps her make a careful choice.

## Book 2: The Cat under the Bed

Target: cat, fur, tail, hide, afraid, quiet, come out.
Recycle: bed, room, food, new.

Mr Lee brings home a small grey cat named Misty. The cat has soft fur, green eyes, and a long tail.

When Misty enters the apartment, she runs under the bed to hide. She does not come out for food or water.

Mr Lee wants to pick her up, but his neighbour says, "She is afraid. Give her time."

They put a clean bowl of water near the bed and leave the room quiet.

After an hour, one grey paw appears. Then Misty's head comes out. She smells the food and walks slowly to the bowl.

That evening, she sits under the sofa. The next morning, she sits on it.

After three days, Misty is calm and sleeps beside Mr Lee. Her tail is outside the blanket.

The apartment did not change. Misty changed because it became familiar. Sometimes a new pet needs quiet more than attention.

## Book 3: A Bird at the Window

Target: bird, wing, feather, fly, hurt, box, wild.
Recycle: window, morning, small, help.

Ana hears a sound against her window. A small bird is on the balcony. One wing is open, and the bird may be hurt because it does not fly away.

Ana puts on gloves and carefully places the bird in a box with air holes. She does not give it bread, milk, or medicine because she does not know what wild birds need.

She calls an animal centre.

"Keep the box dark and quiet," the worker says. "Bring the bird to us."

At the centre, a woman examines the bird's wing. It is not broken. The bird is only shocked and tired, so it needs a quiet place to rest.

After two hours, they take the box outside. The bird stands, moves both wings, and flies into a tree.

Ana finds one small feather in the empty box.

She leaves the feather at the animal centre.

The bird leaves the box by itself. Ana watches until it disappears between the trees.

## Book 4: The Rabbit's New Home

Target: rabbit, cage, hay, clean, chew, space, care.
Recycle: home, food, water, daily.

Leo's family adopts a white rabbit named Snow. The pet shop sells small cages, but an animal-care worker explains that rabbits need space.

The family prepares a safe area in the living room. There is a box for sleeping, a bowl for water, and a place for hay.

Snow likes to chew. On the first day, he finds a computer cable.

"No, Snow!" Leo moves the cable above a shelf.

Every morning, Leo gives Snow fresh hay and water. Twice a week, he cleans the sleeping box.

He also feeds Snow before school, and Snow likes to chew a safe piece of wood.

Snow learns to come when Leo brings food. He also learns where he can run.

The family thought a rabbit only needed a cage and carrots. Now Snow has a safe room to run through, and the computer cable stays on the shelf.

## Book 5: The Pet Photo Day

Target: pet, fish, horse, friendly, active, calm, describe.
Recycle: dog, cat, bird, rabbit, colour.

The local school has a pet photo day. Students bring photographs, not the animals themselves.

Lina shows her orange fish. "They are quiet, but they are always moving." Her second photograph shows a rabbit beside a cage and hay.

The rabbit likes to chew.

Ben has two photographs of a large brown horse. "She is strong, calm, and friendly."

Maya shows Bruno the dog with his lead and bowl. He is friendly and active. Mr Lee sends a picture of Misty the cat, her tail, and a bird with one open wing.

One grey feather lies beside the bird.

One student has no pet. He brings a photograph of birds in the park.

"Are wild birds pets?" someone asks.

"No," the teacher says, "but they are animals we can watch and protect."

The class makes a wall with two parts: `Pets` and `Wild Animals`.

Every student must describe one photograph. The animals look different and need different care. The wall shows that liking animals can mean owning one, helping one, or simply leaving it free.
