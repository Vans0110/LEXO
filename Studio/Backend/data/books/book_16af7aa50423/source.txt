The Bus to River Street

Nora needs to go to fifty-eight River Street. At the bus stop, she sees Route six and Route sixteen on the sign.

"Does Route six go to River Street?" she asks a passenger.

"Yes, but you must change buses at Market Square. Route sixteen is direct."

Route sixteen arrives first. Nora gets on and pays the two-dollar fare. The driver gives her a ticket as the bus turns left.

"Which stop is near number fifty-eight?" Nora asks.

"Get off at River Street Library. It is the sixth stop."

Nora counts the stops. At the fifth, she gets ready. At the sixth, she gets off beside the library.

Number fifty-eight is across the street. The whole trip takes eighteen minutes.

On the way home, Nora takes Route sixteen again. This time, she does not need to ask where to get off.
