# Chapter 1: Introduction

## Цель главы

Представиться, назвать имя, возраст, страну, город и язык, спросить такую же
информацию у другого человека.

## Ядро

- Слова: name, first name, last name, age, country, city, language, student,
  teacher, friend, new, from, live.
- Числа: 0-20, возраст, телефонные цифры, номер комнаты.
- Фразы: `My name is...`, `I am from...`, `I live in...`, `Nice to meet you`,
  `What is your name?`, `How old are you?`
- Грамматика: `I am`, `you are`, `he/she is`, простые вопросы с `be`.

## Book 1: First Day

Target: name, new, student, teacher, friend, from, city, eighteen.

Daniel is eighteen years old. He is from a small city called Westbridge. Today is his first day at a new language school.

Daniel walks into Room twelve. A teacher stands near the door.

"Hello. I am Ms Green," she says. "What is your name?"

"My name is Daniel Ortiz," he answers.

"And what is your age?"

"I am eighteen."

The teacher gives him a name tag. Daniel writes his first name and last name on it. Then he sits next to a girl.

"Hi, I am Kate," the girl says. "I am from Canada."

"Nice to meet you," Daniel says. "I am from Spain, but I live in Westbridge now."

Kate is nineteen. She speaks English and Spanish. Daniel speaks Spanish and a little English.

At lunch, Kate introduces Daniel to two friends. Daniel is nervous at first, but the students are kind. At the end of the day, the school does not feel new anymore.

## Book 2: Two People, One City

Target: country, address, street, number, neighbour, near.
Recycle: name, from, live, city.

Mina is at a bus stop. A young man looks at the street numbers.

"Excuse me," he says. "Is this Pine Street?"

"Yes, it is," Mina answers.

"Thank you. I am Leo. I am new in this city."

"I am Mina. What is your home country?"

"Italy. My new address is twenty-four Pine Street."

Mina laughs. "Really? I live at number twenty, near your building. We are neighbours."

Leo is twenty years old. Mina is nineteen. They both study at City College. Leo studies art, and Mina studies English.

Their bus is number eight, but it is late. They talk while they wait. Mina gives Leo her phone number. He reads the digits back to her, and Mina says they are correct.

When the bus arrives, they sit together. Leo came to the stop as a stranger. Ten minutes later, he has a new neighbour and a new friend.

## Book 3: The Wrong Classroom

Target: room, floor, class, course, right, wrong, upstairs, downstairs.
Recycle: student, teacher, name, number.

Sara is a new student at Hill School. Her English class is in Room fourteen.

She walks into Room forty-one and sits down. There are books, maps, and twelve students in the room. A man writes numbers on the board.

"Good morning," he says. "Welcome to the maths class."

Sara looks at her card. It says: English course, Room fourteen, first floor.

"I am sorry," Sara says. "I am in the wrong room."

The teacher smiles. "Room fourteen is downstairs. This is Room forty-one upstairs, on the fourth floor."

A student shows Sara the stairs. Downstairs, Sara finds the right classroom.

"Are you Sara?" the English teacher asks.

"Yes, I am."

"Welcome, Sara. Please sit next to Amir."

Sara is five minutes late, but she is now in the right class. She checks every room number carefully for the rest of the day.

## Book 4: A Voice from Online

Target: phone, email, call, spell, repeat, speak, understand.
Recycle: name, country, language, number.

Yuki is in Japan, and Ben is in Australia. They are language partners. Today is their first online call.

Ben calls at seven o'clock. A friendly face appears on Yuki's phone.

"Hello! Am I speaking to Yuki?"

"Yes. And you are Ben?"

"That is right. Nice to meet you."

They talk about their countries and cities. Ben speaks slowly. When Yuki does not understand, she asks, "Can you repeat that, please?"

At the end, Ben gives Yuki his email address.

"Can you spell your first name and last name?" Yuki asks.

"WALKER," Ben says.

Yuki writes it down and reads it back. Then she gives Ben her phone number. They check every digit.

The call is only fifteen minutes long, but both students use real English. They plan another call for next Tuesday.

## Book 5: Welcome to Maple Street

Target: welcome, form, first, last, apartment, information.
Recycle: name, age, country, city, language, address, phone.

Nora moves into Apartment six at fifteen Maple Street. In the entrance hall, she finds a small welcome table.

Mr Lee, an older neighbour, sits behind the table.

"Welcome to Maple Street," he says. "We have a contact form for new neighbours."

The form asks for basic information. Nora writes her first name, last name, age, apartment number, phone number, and email address.

"Where are you from?" Mr Lee asks.

"I am from Ukraine. I am twenty years old, and I am a university student."

"What languages do you speak?"

"Ukrainian, English, and a little Polish."

Two neighbours come into the hall. Mr Lee introduces them to Nora. One is a nurse from Apartment four. The other is a cook from Apartment nine.

Later, Nora puts the welcome card on her table. It has useful phone numbers and the names of her neighbours. Her apartment is new, but the building already feels friendly.
