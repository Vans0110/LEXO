The Hottest Afternoon

It is July, in the summer season, and the temperature is thirty-six degrees. The sun is bright while Maya and Ben walk through the park.

The grass is dry, and there are no clouds in the sky. Ben has a hat, but Maya does not.

"I am very hot and thirsty," Maya says.

They sit in the shade under a large tree. Ben gives Maya some water from his bottle.

A sign says the small cafe is three hundred metres away. They walk slowly and stop again near a fountain.

At the cafe, Maya buys a cold drink and a light hat. They decide not to play tennis.

Instead, they rest inside until five o'clock. The temperature falls to thirty-one degrees.

On the way home, a cool wind begins.

"Next time, we check the weather before we leave," Ben says.

Maya touches her new hat. "And I bring my own water."
