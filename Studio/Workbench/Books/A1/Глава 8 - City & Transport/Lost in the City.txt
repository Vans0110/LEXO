Lost in the City

Ethan visits a large city for the first time. He wants to meet Daniel at the train station.

His phone map shows the way, but after ten minutes the battery is dead. Ethan is lost. A bus, taxi, and bicycle pass him. He walks down King Street, crosses a small bridge, and turns at the wrong corner.

At last, he sees a police officer.

"Excuse me. Can you help me find Central Station?"

"Yes. Go straight for two blocks. Turn left at the bank, cross River Road, and you will see the station."

Ethan repeats the directions. "Straight, left at the bank, then across River Road."

He follows the route carefully. Soon he sees a large clock above the station door. Daniel is waiting outside.

"You are twenty minutes late," Daniel says.

"My phone is dead, but I can still ask for directions."

Before they leave, Ethan buys a small paper map. It does not need a battery.
