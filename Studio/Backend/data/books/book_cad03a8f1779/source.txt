The Wrong Classroom

Sara is a new student at Hill School. Her English class is in Room fourteen.

She walks into Room forty-one and sits down. There are books, maps, and twelve students in the room. A man writes numbers on the board.

"Good morning," he says. "Welcome to the maths class."

Sara looks at her card. It says: English course, Room fourteen, first floor.

"I am sorry," Sara says. "I am in the wrong room."

The teacher smiles. "Room fourteen is downstairs. This is Room forty-one upstairs, on the fourth floor."

A student shows Sara the stairs. Downstairs, Sara finds the right classroom.

"Are you Sara?" the English teacher asks.

"Yes, I am."

"Welcome, Sara. Please sit next to Amir."

Sara is five minutes late, but she is now in the right class. She checks every room number carefully for the rest of the day.
