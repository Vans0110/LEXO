# Chapter 15: Friends & Emotions

## Цель главы

Назвать основные эмоции, объяснить простую причину и поддержать друга.

## Ядро

- Слова: happy, sad, angry, afraid, worried, nervous, excited, surprised,
  tired, calm, lonely, proud, sorry, help, listen, understand, problem.
- Грамматика: `feel`, `because`, `so`, простые причины и реакции.

## Book 1: A Bad Day for Nora

Target: sad, tired, worried, listen, problem, better.
Recycle: friend, work, bus, home.

Nora arrives at work late because her bus breaks down. Then she drops coffee on an important paper. At lunch, she receives a message: her weekend trip is cancelled.

Nora feels worried, lonely, sad, and tired. Her friend Amir is busy too, but he sees that she is quiet.

"Are you okay?"

"It is only a bad day," Nora says, but her eyes look sad.

Amir does not give quick advice. He sits with her and listens while she explains each problem.

Nora is nervous about the weekend, but talking to Amir helps.

They print the paper again. Amir helps her find another bus for the weekend. The trip is not possible, but they plan a local walk instead.

After work, Nora still feels tired, but not alone.

"Thank you for listening," she says.

"I did not fix the whole day."

"No," Nora answers, "but you made the end of it better."

## Book 2: The Unanswered Message

Target: upset, busy, answer, ignore, reason, explain.
Recycle: message, phone, friend, wait.

Lily sends her friend Max a message on Monday. He does not answer.

On Tuesday, she sends another. The message shows that Max read it, but there is still no reply.

Lily feels upset, angry, and lonely. She thinks Max is ignoring her.

On Wednesday, she sees him outside the library. Max is busy with a class, but he stops to explain.

"Are you angry with me?" she asks.

Max looks surprised. He shows her his phone. The screen is broken, and he can read messages but cannot type or call. Lily begins to understand.

"I wanted to answer," he says. "I was going to visit you after class."

Lily feels sorry because she judged him too quickly. Max accepts her apology, and they go to a phone shop together.

Later, Max sends one short message from his repaired phone: `It works!`

Lily replies at once. A quiet phone can have many reasons. It does not always mean a quiet friendship.

## Book 3: Before the Presentation

Target: nervous, afraid, calm, breathe, practise, proud.
Recycle: class, project, speak, friend.

Ken must give a short presentation in English. Before class, his hands feel cold, and he cannot remember the first sentence.

"I am too nervous," he tells Rosa.

Rosa listens, then takes him to an empty classroom. "First, breathe slowly. Then say only the first line."

Ken says it. They practise the next line, then the whole first minute.

In class, Ken is still afraid, but he looks at Rosa. She smiles.

He speaks slowly and uses his notes. He makes one small mistake, corrects it, and continues.

When he finishes, the teacher asks two questions. Ken answers both.

Outside, he feels calm, happy, and proud again.

"You did it," Rosa says.

"I was nervous the whole time."

"Being brave does not mean you are never afraid."

Ken looks at the notes still shaking in his hand and smiles. He finished it.

## Book 4: Two Tickets, Three Friends

Target: disappointed, fair, choose, include, agree, next time.
Recycle: ticket, film, friend, price.

Sara wins two free concert tickets. She wants to go with both Mina and Joel, but there are only two seats.

Mina loves the band. Joel helped Sara enter the competition. Sara does not know whom to choose.

When she explains the problem, both friends feel disappointed, surprised, and upset.

"We can choose with a coin," Joel says.

Mina shakes her head. "That is fair, but maybe there is another idea."

They check the concert website. One more ticket is available, but it costs forty dollars.

Sara offers to pay half. Mina and Joel each pay ten dollars, and Sara pays twenty. Now all three can go, and they feel excited.

The seats are not together. Sara and Mina sit in one area, and Joel sits nearby.

The plan can include everyone. They agree that Joel will choose next time. When the concert starts, he waves from the next area, and Sara and Mina wave back.

## Book 5: The Apology Note

Target: angry, sorry, mistake, apology, forgive, honest, calm.
Recycle: roommate, cup, kitchen, message.

Ben uses his roommate's favourite cup and drops it. The cup breaks.

Ben is afraid to tell Maya. He puts the pieces in a box and leaves for work.

All day, he feels worried. In the evening, Maya sees the empty place in the cupboard.

"Where is my yellow cup?"

Ben first wants to hide his mistake and say he does not know. Then he takes out the box and stays calm.

"I broke it this morning. I am sorry. I should have told you at once."

Maya is angry, disappointed, sad, and tired because the cup was a gift from her sister. Ben gives her a short apology note and offers to find a similar cup.

The next day, they visit three shops. They do not find the same one, but Maya chooses a new blue cup.

"I forgive you," she says. Ben is excited and happy to hear that. "I understand it was a mistake. Please be honest sooner next time."

At home, Maya puts the blue cup in the old yellow cup's place. This time, Ben asks before he uses it.
