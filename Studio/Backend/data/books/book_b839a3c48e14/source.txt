The Phone Fund

Lina's goal is a phone that costs two hundred and forty dollars. She has one hundred and twenty dollars.

She makes a simple savings plan. Each week, she saves fifteen dollars in a blue box. She writes the amount on a chart.

Week one: one hundred and thirty-five dollars. Week two: one hundred and fifty dollars. Week three: one hundred and sixty-five dollars.

Sometimes Lina wants to spend the money on coffee or clothes. She asks herself, "Do I need this today?"

After eight weeks, the box has two hundred and forty dollars. At the shop, the phone is now on sale for two hundred and twenty, and Lina pays by card.

Lina buys it and still has twenty dollars.

She does not spend the extra money. She starts a new line on the chart: `Bicycle fund — twenty dollars`.

Saving is slow, but Lina likes watching a small amount become enough. The chart helps her see every step.
