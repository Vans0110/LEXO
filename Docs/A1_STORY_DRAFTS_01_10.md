# Глава 1 — Introduction

## First Day

Daniel is eighteen years old. He lives in a small city with his parents. Today is his first day at a new school.

In the morning, Daniel feels nervous. He does not know the teachers or students. He takes his bag and goes to school by bus.

In the classroom, a girl smiles at him.

“Hello! My name is Kate,” she says.

Daniel smiles too. “Hi. I am Daniel.”

Kate shows him the classroom and helps him find his books. At lunch time, Daniel sits with Kate and her friends. They talk about games and music.

The students are friendly, and the teachers are kind. Daniel feels much better now.

In the evening, he goes home and talks to his parents.

“My first day was very good,” he says happily.

# Глава 2 — Family

## Grandma’s Dinner

Emma has a big family. Every Sunday, her family visits her grandmother.

Today, Emma and her parents go to her grandmother’s house in the afternoon. Her grandmother cooks chicken, rice, and vegetables. The food smells very good.

Emma helps set the table. Her younger brother brings juice and bread.

During dinner, the family talks and laughs. Emma’s grandfather tells funny stories about his old job. Everyone listens carefully.

After dinner, Emma looks at old family photos with her grandmother. There are pictures of birthdays, holidays, and small children.

Emma likes these evenings very much. She feels warm and happy with her family.

At night, Emma says goodbye and hugs her grandparents.

“See you next Sunday!” she says.

# Глава 3 — Home & Furniture

## New Apartment

Oliver moves into a new apartment in the city. The apartment is small but comfortable.

There is a sofa near the window and a table in the kitchen. Oliver puts his books on a shelf and his clothes in a wardrobe.

In the bedroom, there is a bed and a small lamp. Oliver likes the yellow walls because they make the room feel warm.

After work, Oliver goes to a furniture shop and buys two chairs. The chairs are simple but nice.

In the evening, his friend Mia visits the apartment.

“I really like your new home,” she says.

Oliver smiles. He feels happy because everything is clean and quiet. They drink tea and talk for a long time.

Before sleep, Oliver sits on the sofa and reads a book. It feels like home now.

# Глава 4 — Food & Drinks

## The Cooking Problem

Liam wants to cook dinner for his friend Sophie. He buys tomatoes, cheese, bread, and juice at a small shop near his house.

At home, Liam starts cooking. He cuts vegetables and puts them into a pan. But suddenly, he smells something strange.

“Oh no!” Liam says.

The bread is black because it stays in the oven too long.

Liam feels embarrassed, but Sophie laughs when she arrives.

“It’s okay,” she says kindly. “We can cook together.”

They make a new dinner with pasta and salad. Sophie shows Liam how to cook the sauce correctly.

Later, they sit at the table and eat together. The food is very good.

“This is much better,” Liam says happily.

Sophie smiles. “Cooking is easier with two people.”

# Глава 5 — Work & Study

## Late for Class

Mia is a university student. Every morning, she takes the bus to class.

Today, Mia wakes up late. She looks at the clock and quickly gets out of bed.

“Oh no! I am late!” she says.

She puts on her clothes, takes her bag, and runs outside. But the bus leaves before she arrives.

Mia feels stressed. She waits for the next bus and checks her phone many times.

When she finally arrives at the university, the lesson already starts. Mia quietly opens the classroom door.

The teacher looks at her but smiles.

“Good morning, Mia,” he says.

“Sorry I am late,” Mia answers.

After class, Mia studies with her friends in the library. They prepare for an important exam next week.

In the evening, Mia goes home tired but calm.

# Глава 6 — Daily Routine & Time

## The Bus Driver

Nick is a bus driver in a big city. Every day, he wakes up at five thirty in the morning.

He drinks tea, eats eggs, and goes to work. Nick likes quiet mornings before the streets become busy.

At six thirty, he starts driving the bus. Many people get on the bus: students, workers, and old people.

Nick always says, “Good morning!” to the passengers.

At lunch time, Nick stops near a small café. He eats soup and drinks coffee. Then he goes back to work.

In the evening, the traffic is very slow. There are many cars and people on the streets.

Nick feels tired after work, but he likes helping people travel around the city.

At home, he listens to music and sits quietly before sleep.

# Глава 7 — Clothes & Shopping

## The Blue Jacket

Anna wants a new jacket for winter. Her old jacket is too small now.

After work, she goes to a clothing shop in the city center. There are many jackets in different colors: black, green, red, and blue.

Anna tries on a black jacket first, but she does not like it. Then she sees a blue jacket near the window.

The jacket is warm and very comfortable.

A shop worker smiles and says, “It looks very good on you.”

Anna checks the price carefully. The jacket is not cheap, but she really likes it.

Finally, she buys the jacket and walks home happily.

Outside, the weather is cold and snowy. Anna wears her new jacket immediately.

“This is perfect for winter,” she thinks.

# Глава 8 — City & Transport

## Lost in the City

Ethan visits a big city for the first time. He wants to meet his friend near the train station.

But the city is very busy. There are many streets, buses, and tall buildings. Ethan looks at his phone, but the battery is dead.

He walks for twenty minutes and feels confused.

Finally, he sees a police officer.

“Excuse me,” Ethan says. “Can you help me find the train station?”

The officer smiles and gives directions.

“Go straight, then turn left near the bank,” he says.

Ethan thanks him and follows the directions carefully.

Soon he sees the station and his friend Daniel waiting outside.

“You are late!” Daniel laughs.

Ethan smiles tiredly.

“This city is like a maze,” he says.

# Глава 9 — Weather & Nature

## A Rainy Day

Today, the weather is terrible. The sky is dark, and heavy rain falls all morning.

Julia wants to go to the park with her friend, but now she stays at home. She sits near the window and watches the rain.

After lunch, Julia hears thunder outside. Her small dog becomes afraid and hides under the table.

“It’s okay,” Julia says softly.

She sits with the dog and reads a book for some time.

In the evening, the rain stops. The sky becomes orange and pink.

Julia goes outside for a short walk. The streets are wet, and the air feels fresh and cold.

She sees water on the trees and flowers. Everything looks clean and quiet after the storm.

Julia smiles.

“Rainy days are not always bad,” she thinks.

# Глава 10 — Numbers & Money

## The Lost Wallet

Chris goes to a market to buy fruit and vegetables. He takes fifty dollars with him.

At the market, he buys apples, bananas, tomatoes, and bread. Then he wants to buy coffee.

But when Chris looks in his pocket, his wallet is gone.

“Oh no,” he says quietly.

Chris walks around the market and looks everywhere. He feels worried because his money and bank card are inside the wallet.

Ten minutes later, an old woman walks to him.

“Is this your wallet?” she asks.

Chris looks at the wallet happily.

“Yes! Thank you very much!”

Everything is still inside: the money, cards, and photos.

Chris smiles and buys coffee for the woman.

“That is very kind of you,” she says.

Chris goes home relieved and thankful.
