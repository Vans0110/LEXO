Max lives in a big city. He is a student. Every morning, Max gets up at seven o’clock. He drinks hot coffee and eats a sandwich for breakfast. He likes quiet mornings before his busy day starts.

Max has a dog. Its name is Rex. Rex is very friendly and happy. In the morning, they go to the park together. In the park, there are many tall trees and beautiful flowers. Max plays with Rex for thirty minutes, and then he goes to the university.

At the university, Max studies history. He has two best friends: Anna and Ivan. After lessons, they usually go to a small cafe. The friends drink green tea and talk about their lives. Anna wants to be a doctor, and Ivan wants to be an architect.

In the evening, Max goes home. He cooks a simple dinner and watches TV. Sometimes he reads an interesting book or listens to classical music. At eleven o’clock, Max goes to bed. He is happy because he has good friends and a loyal dog.
