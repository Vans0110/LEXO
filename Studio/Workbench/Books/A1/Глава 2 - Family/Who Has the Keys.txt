Who Has the Keys?

The Green family is ready to leave the house, but the car keys are not on the hall table.

"Whose keys are these?" Ben asks. "And whose small silver key is this?"

"That one is mine," Grandma says. "It is for my apartment."

Mother looks in her bag. She is Father's wife. Father checks his coat pockets. Their daughter, Ben's quiet sister, looks under the sofa.

"Dad often puts the car keys near his glasses," she says.

His glasses are on the kitchen table, but the keys are not there.

Then the family dog walks into the room. Its funny eyes look at Ben, and something makes a small sound on its collar.

"The keys!" Ben says.

The car keys are on the dog's collar. Father put them there when both hands were full.

"Those keys are yours," Mother tells him, "so please be more careful."

The parents and children leave ten minutes late. This time, Father keeps the keys in his own pocket.
