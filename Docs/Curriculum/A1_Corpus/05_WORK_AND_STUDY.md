# Chapter 5: Work & Study

## Цель главы

Рассказать о работе или учёбе, профессиях, расписании и учебных предметах.

## Ядро

- Слова: job, work, study, school, university, office, classroom, library,
  teacher, student, doctor, nurse, driver, cook, lesson, course, exam.
- Предметы: pen, pencil, notebook, book, folder, ruler, backpack, computer.
- Грамматика: Present Simple, вопросы с `do/does`.

## Book 1: New Student

Target: university, course, lesson, schedule, classroom, library, notebook.
Recycle: name, room number, time, friend.

Mia is a new student at North University. She studies on an English course.

On Monday, she checks her schedule. Her first lesson starts at nine in Room twenty-two. She puts a notebook, two pens, and a book in her backpack.

At the university, Mia cannot find Room twenty-two.

"Do you study English?" a student asks.

"Yes, I do. I am looking for my classroom."

"I study English too. Come with me."

His name is Alex. He shows Mia the classroom and introduces her to the teacher.

After the lesson, Alex takes Mia to the library. There are computers near the door and study tables by the windows.

"Do you study here every day?" Mia asks.

"Not every day. I usually come on Tuesday and Thursday."

Mia writes the library hours, the date of her first exam, and her first class project in her notebook. The university is large, but now she knows one classroom, one library, and one friendly student.

## Book 2: The English Teacher's Bag

Target: pen, pencil, folder, ruler, dictionary, belong, return.
Recycle: teacher, student, book, bag, colour.

After class, Mr Brown leaves a black bag under his classroom desk. Five students find it.

"Does this bag belong to Mr Brown?" Eva asks.

"Yes," Tom says. "His name is on the folder."

They open the bag to find a phone number. Inside there are three blue pens, two pencils, a red folder, a ruler, a small dictionary, and a book.

"Teachers carry a lot of things," Eva says.

The dictionary has the school office number on the first page. Tom calls the office.

Mr Brown returns ten minutes later.

"Thank you," he says. "I need that folder for my next lesson."

He checks the bag. Everything is there.

The next day, Mr Brown writes a new sentence on the board: `These students are careful and kind.`

The class knows exactly who he means.

## Book 3: A Day at the Bakery

Target: bakery, baker, customer, open, close, shift, make, sell.
Recycle: bread, price, time, morning.

Nora works at a bakery. Her morning shift starts at six o'clock.

First, she opens the shop and turns on the lights. A baker makes bread in the kitchen. Nora puts bread and cakes on the shelves.

The bakery opens to customers at seven and closes at six. It sells bread, cakes, and breakfast to a doctor, nurse, driver, cleaner, cook, and mechanic before work.

"How much is this loaf?" a customer asks.

"It is three dollars," Nora answers.

At ten, Nora checks a list of orders. One customer needs a birthday cake at two o'clock. Another needs twelve rolls at four.

Nora does not make the cakes, but she talks to customers, takes payments, and packs orders.

Her shift ends at two. Before she leaves, she gives the afternoon worker the order list.

"Do you like this job?" he asks.

"Yes," Nora says. "The mornings are early, but the shop always smells good."

## Book 4: The Library Table

Target: project, laptop, notes, question, answer, practise, together.
Recycle: library, book, pen, study, schedule.

Four university students have a science project for their course. They meet at a library table after school at four o'clock.

Jamal brings a laptop computer and a backpack. Rosa brings two books. Ken has a folder with notebook pages, but Lily has only one pen and one pencil.

"Do we have everything?" Jamal asks.

"We do not have a ruler," Ken says.

Lily borrows one from the library desk. A guard shows her where to sign.

The students read a question in their project book. They do not know the answer, so Rosa finds it in a dictionary. Jamal writes it on the laptop.

At six, they study and practise their short presentation. Each student speaks for one minute, as in an exam.

"We work well together," Lily says.

They put their books, notes, ruler, and pens back into their bags. The project is not finished, but everyone knows what to do before the next meeting.

## Book 5: Who Works at Night?

Target: profession, nurse, guard, cleaner, mechanic, night, day.
Recycle: work, job, driver, cook, family, schedule.

Five friends talk about their families' jobs.

"My mother is a nurse," Priya says. "She often works at night."

"Does she work with a doctor?" Ben asks.

"Yes, every night."

"Does she sleep in the day?" Ben asks.

"Yes. She comes home at eight in the morning."

Ben's father is a security guard. He starts work at ten in the evening. Lucy's sister is a cleaner at an office. She works very early, before the office workers arrive.

Omar's brother is a mechanic. He usually works in the day, but sometimes he helps drivers at night. Ana's husband is a baker and cook in a hotel restaurant. His shift ends at eleven.

The friends make a simple chart with names, professions, and work times. Each profession has a different schedule.

"Many people work while we sleep," Lucy says.

That night, she notices the bus driver, the nurse at the hospital, and the guard near her building. The city has a different team after dark.
