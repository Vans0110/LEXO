First Day

Daniel is eighteen years old. He is from a small city called Westbridge. Today is his first day at a new language school.

Daniel walks into Room twelve. A teacher stands near the door.

"Hello. I am Ms Green," she says. "What is your name?"

"My name is Daniel Ortiz," he answers.

"And what is your age?"

"I am eighteen."

The teacher gives him a name tag. Daniel writes his first name and last name on it. Then he sits next to a girl.

"Hi, I am Kate," the girl says. "I am from Canada."

"Nice to meet you," Daniel says. "I am from Spain, but I live in Westbridge now."

Kate is nineteen. She speaks English and Spanish. Daniel speaks Spanish and a little English.

At lunch, Kate introduces Daniel to two friends. Daniel is nervous at first, but the students are kind. At the end of the day, the school does not feel new anymore.
