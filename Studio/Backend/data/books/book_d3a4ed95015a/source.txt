New Student

Mia is a new student at North University. She studies on an English course.

On Monday, she checks her schedule. Her first lesson starts at nine in Room twenty-two. She puts a notebook, two pens, and a book in her backpack.

At the university, Mia cannot find Room twenty-two.

"Do you study English?" a student asks.

"Yes, I do. I am looking for my classroom."

"I study English too. Come with me."

His name is Alex. He shows Mia the classroom and introduces her to the teacher.

After the lesson, Alex takes Mia to the library. There are computers near the door and study tables by the windows.

"Do you study here every day?" Mia asks.

"Not every day. I usually come on Tuesday and Thursday."

Mia writes the library hours, the date of her first exam, and her first class project in her notebook. The university is large, but now she knows one classroom, one library, and one friendly student.
