The Bus Driver

Nick is a bus driver in a large city. From Monday to Friday, he wakes up at five thirty. He washes, gets dressed, and eats two eggs with toast.

Nick leaves home at six. His route starts at six thirty. Before he drives, he checks the bus, the lights, and the route sheet.

The first passengers are usually nurses, cleaners, and students. Nick always says, "Good morning."

At ten fifteen, he has a twenty-minute break. He drinks coffee at a small cafe near Stop fourteen. At twelve, the streets become busy.

Nick finishes work at three in the afternoon. Sometimes traffic makes him late, but today he arrives home at three forty.

In the evening, he cooks dinner and listens to music. He usually goes to bed at nine thirty. His working day begins early, so his quiet evening ends early too.
