# Chapter 19: Dreams & Future

## Цель главы

Рассказать о мечте, цели и простом будущем намерении.

## Ядро

- Слова: dream, future, goal, hope, plan, become, improve, learn, move, create,
  save, someday, next year, possible.
- Грамматика: `want to`, `would like to`, `going to`.

## Book 1: The Dream Job Wall

Target: dream job, become, future, skill, help, design.
Recycle: work, study, profession, picture.

A teacher asks every student to add one future dream job to a classroom wall.

Mina wants to become a nurse because she likes helping people. Joel wants to design buildings. Sara would like to teach music. Ben is not sure.

"Can I write more than one job?" he asks.

"Of course. A dream can change," the teacher says.

Ben draws a camera, a computer, and a train. He likes taking photographs, using technology, and travelling.

Under the pictures, he writes: `I want a job with stories and places.`

His goal is to learn how good design can help people.

The teacher suggests journalism. Ben does not know much about it, so he plans to visit the school newspaper.

The wall does not tell students exactly which profession they will choose. Someday their dream job may change, but they can improve and create new skills.

Ben leaves space under his pictures for a new idea.

## Book 2: Five Dollars a Week

Target: goal, save, future, each, reach, possible.
Recycle: money, week, plan, bicycle.

Ana wants to buy a bicycle next year. A good used bicycle costs about two hundred and sixty dollars.

She has twenty dollars now. Ana decides to save five dollars each week.

She makes a chart with forty-eight boxes. Every Friday, she puts five dollars in a jar and colours one box.

After ten weeks, she has seventy dollars. The goal still looks far away.

Her uncle offers to give her the rest, but Ana says, "I want to reach the goal myself."

She begins helping a neighbour in the garden twice a month. The neighbour pays her ten dollars each time.

Now Ana can save more than five dollars some weeks.

The bicycle is still in the future, but the plan makes it possible. Ana hopes to improve the plan, and each coloured box is a small part of a larger dream.

## Book 3: A Cafe of Her Own

Target: someday, own, open, create, menu, customer, improve.
Recycle: cafe, cook, money, work.

Rosa works in a busy cafe. Someday, she wants to open a small cafe of her own.

Her hope is to create a place where every customer feels welcome.

She keeps a notebook of ideas. One page has simple breakfast menus. Another has drawings of tables and plants.

Rosa is good at cooking, but she needs to improve her knowledge of money and business. Next year, she is going to take an evening course.

Her manager lets her create one new soup for the weekly menu. Customers like it, and several ask for it again.

"This is good practice for your future cafe," the manager says.

Rosa saves part of every salary. She also asks customers what they enjoy.

She may move to a larger cafe first.

Her own cafe does not exist yet. Maybe in ten years it will welcome many customers. There is no building, sign, or door today. But there is a notebook, a savings account, a new course, and one popular soup. The dream already has its first pieces.

Rosa knows the plan is possible.

## Book 4: The City I Want to See

Target: visit, travel, learn, culture, language, next year.
Recycle: city, map, hotel, save.

Leo wants to visit Kyoto. He has photographs of its streets, temples, and gardens above his desk. His journey will begin at an airport, so he checks his passport, luggage, suitcase weight, check-in time, plane seat, and departure gate.

He is going to travel next spring. The trip is nine months away.

Leo starts learning simple Japanese phrases. He saves fifty dollars each month and reads about local transport.

His friend asks, "Why do you plan so early?"

"Because I do not only want to see the city. I want to understand a little of its culture."

He also wants to learn how daily life and culture can change from place to place.

Leo chooses a small hotel near a train station. The distance from the beach and mountains is written on the map. At reception, each guest gets a key card. Later, a train carriage will depart for the hills. He also finds a language exchange partner online.

Every Sunday, they use a laptop, microphone, camera, and video call to practise for twenty minutes. Leo teaches English, and his partner teaches Japanese.

Next year, Leo is going to stand in a city that is now only on his wall. His journey begins long before the plane leaves.

## Book 5: Our Plans in Ten Years

Target: in ten years, hope, maybe, still, change, future.
Recycle: friends, job, home, travel.

Five friends meet in the park and talk about life in ten years.

Maya hopes to be a doctor. Ben wants to live near the sea. Lina would like to have a family and a large kitchen. Omar plans to travel for his work.

Ben may move to the coast, and Omar may move between cities.

Sara is quiet.

"Do you have a plan?" Maya asks.

"Maybe," Sara says. "I hope I still make music, but I do not know where I will live or work."

The friends write their ideas on five cards and put them in a box. Ben keeps the box.

"We will open it in ten years."

They know many plans will change. A different job, home, or city may become important.

The cards are not promises. They are pictures of what the friends hope for today. The future can change, but having a direction helps them take the next step.
