# Chapter 6: Daily Routine & Time

## Цель главы

Описать обычный день, назвать время и частоту действий, понять расписание.

## Ядро

- Слова: wake up, get up, wash, dress, start, finish, leave, arrive, early,
  late, morning, afternoon, evening, night, always, usually, sometimes, never.
- Данные: часы, минуты, дни недели, рабочие и выходные дни.
- Грамматика: Present Simple, время, наречия частоты.

## Book 1: The Bus Driver

Target: wake up, early, start, finish, passenger, route, break, traffic.
Recycle: driver, bus, work, morning, time.

Nick is a bus driver in a large city. From Monday to Friday, he wakes up at five thirty. He washes, gets dressed, and eats two eggs with toast.

Nick leaves home at six. His route starts at six thirty. Before he drives, he checks the bus, the lights, and the route sheet.

The first passengers are usually nurses, cleaners, and students. Nick always says, "Good morning."

At ten fifteen, he has a twenty-minute break. He drinks coffee at a small cafe near Stop fourteen. At twelve, the streets become busy.

Nick finishes work at three in the afternoon. Sometimes traffic makes him late, but today he arrives home at three forty.

In the evening, he cooks dinner and listens to music. He usually goes to bed at nine thirty. His working day begins early, so his quiet evening ends early too.

## Book 2: Seven O'Clock Again

Target: alarm, get up, shower, breakfast, usually, sometimes, never.
Recycle: clock, morning, work, study.

Two friends, Ava and Kim, share an apartment. Their alarms ring at seven o'clock, but their mornings are very different.

Ava gets up at once. She takes a shower, gets dressed, and makes breakfast. She usually eats cereal and fruit. At seven forty-five, she leaves for work.

Kim turns off the alarm and stays in bed.

"It is seven again," Ava says.

"Five more minutes," Kim answers.

Kim never gets up after only one alarm. She sometimes uses three. At seven thirty, she finally goes to the bathroom. She does not have time for breakfast, so she takes a banana.

Both friends need to leave before eight. Ava is always ready first.

On weekdays, Kim often sleeps late, while Ava rarely does. At night before the weekend, Kim puts her alarm across the room. In the morning, she must stand up to turn it off. For the first time that week, she eats breakfast with Ava.

## Book 3: A Very Busy Morning

Target: list, first, next, before, after, hurry, ready, appointment.
Recycle: time, breakfast, shop, bus.

Leo has a doctor's appointment at eleven. Before he leaves, he has five jobs to do.

He writes a list:

First, wash the dishes. Second, call Grandma. Third, buy milk. Fourth, send an email. Fifth, take the dog outside.

At eight thirty, Leo eats breakfast. First, he washes the dishes. Next, he calls Grandma, but she talks for twenty minutes.

At nine forty, Leo walks the dog. After that, he goes to the shop. There is a long line, and he starts to hurry.

He arrives home at ten thirty. He sends the email, puts the milk in the fridge, and checks his list.

At ten forty, Leo is ready and leaves for the appointment. The bus comes at ten forty-five. He arrives at the doctor's office at ten fifty-eight.

He does not take a break, but he finishes the list and is not late. His morning is busy, but every job is complete.

## Book 4: Sunday without an Alarm

Target: weekday, weekend, rest, slowly, often, rarely, free.
Recycle: wake up, breakfast, morning, family.

During the week, Maria wakes up at six fifteen. On Sunday, she does not use an alarm.

She wakes up slowly and gets up at eight thirty. The apartment is quiet. Her husband is making tea, and their daughter is still asleep.

Maria often works on Saturday, so Sunday is her only free day. The weekend gives her time to rest. She rarely makes a plan for the morning.

At nine, the family has a long breakfast. On weekdays, they eat quickly. On Sunday, they talk, make more tea, and share fruit and bread.

After breakfast, Maria reads by the window. At eleven, the family walks to the park. They do not look at the clock.

In the afternoon, Maria prepares clothes and food for Monday. At ten in the evening, she sets her alarm again.

Sunday is not empty. It simply has a slower schedule.

## Book 5: The Clock in the Kitchen

Target: fast, slow, minute, correct, meeting, miss, fix.
Recycle: clock, late, schedule, leave.

At night, the clock in Jamie's kitchen is ten minutes slow. Jamie does not know this.

On Tuesday, he looks at the clock. It says eight twenty. His meeting starts at nine, and the office is thirty minutes away.

Jamie leaves at eight twenty-five. At the bus stop, his phone says eight thirty-five.

"Which time is correct?" he asks.

The phone is correct. Jamie takes the next bus, but traffic is heavy. He arrives at nine five and misses the start of the meeting.

That evening, Jamie checks every clock in the apartment. The bedroom clock is five minutes fast. The kitchen clock is ten minutes slow. His phone and computer show the same time.

Jamie never wants to miss another meeting. He fixes both clocks without hurrying. Then he writes tomorrow's meeting time on a note: `ten o'clock — leave at nine twenty`.

The next day, he arrives ten minutes early.
