Market Day

Rosa has a twenty-dollar budget for the market. She needs one kilo of potatoes, half a kilo of tomatoes, six eggs, and twelve apples.

The seller weighs the potatoes and tomatoes. They cost five dollars together. A dozen apples costs six dollars. Six eggs cost three dollars.

"What is the total?" Rosa asks.

"Fourteen dollars."

Rosa compares the prices, counts her cash and coins, and pays. The seller gives her six dollars in change and a receipt.

At another table, strawberries cost seven dollars. They are not cheap and are too expensive for Rosa because she has only six dollars left.

She buys a smaller box for four dollars and keeps two dollars for the bus.

At home, Rosa checks the receipt and her shopping list. She has every needed item, one extra box of fruit, and exactly enough money for the journey home.
