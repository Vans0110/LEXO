Seven O'Clock Again

Two friends, Ava and Kim, share an apartment. Their alarms ring at seven o'clock, but their mornings are very different.

Ava gets up at once. She takes a shower, gets dressed, and makes breakfast. She usually eats cereal and fruit. At seven forty-five, she leaves for work.

Kim turns off the alarm and stays in bed.

"It is seven again," Ava says.

"Five more minutes," Kim answers.

Kim never gets up after only one alarm. She sometimes uses three. At seven thirty, she finally goes to the bathroom. She does not have time for breakfast, so she takes a banana.

Both friends need to leave before eight. Ava is always ready first.

On weekdays, Kim often sleeps late, while Ava rarely does. At night before the weekend, Kim puts her alarm across the room. In the morning, she must stand up to turn it off. For the first time that week, she eats breakfast with Ava.
