The Bag at the Airport

Maya is flying to Rome. At check-in, the weight of her blue suitcase is eighteen kilos. The worker puts a tag with Maya's name and flight number on it.

The worker weighs it again before it moves to the plane. Maya keeps her passport and a small red scarf in her hand luggage.

After the flight, Maya waits beside the luggage belt. Her passport is in her hand. A similar blue suitcase appears. It is the same size as hers.

She takes it, but the tag says `Martin Cole`.

"This is not my bag," she says.

A man nearby is holding another blue suitcase. He checks its tag.

"And this is not mine."

They exchange the bags. Maya opens a small side pocket and finds her red scarf. Now she is sure.

Before leaving the airport, she ties the scarf around the handle.

On her return flight, many suitcases are blue, black, and grey. Maya can see her own bag immediately. A small bright detail prevents another mistake.
