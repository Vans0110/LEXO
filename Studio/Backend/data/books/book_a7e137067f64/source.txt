The Smallest Map

Ada has a very small city map. It fits inside her ticket holder and shows the full metro route.

She is at South Station and wants to visit North Museum. The map shows two metro lines: blue and yellow.

Ada takes the blue line from Platform two. After four stops, she changes at City Hall. Signs point to the yellow line upstairs.

"Which platform goes north?" she asks a worker.

"Platform five."

The yellow train arrives in two minutes. North Museum is the third stop.

The fare is three dollars, and the journey takes twenty-five minutes. When Ada leaves the station, the museum is on her right.

Her small map has no pictures of restaurants or shops. It only has lines, stations, and numbers. That is enough.

On the return journey, Ada helps another visitor change from the yellow line to the blue one.
