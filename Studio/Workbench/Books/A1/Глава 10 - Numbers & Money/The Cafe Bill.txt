The Cafe Bill

Four friends eat at a cafe. Their food costs:

Maya: eight dollars. Jon: ten dollars. Sara: nine dollars. Lee: eleven dollars.

The bill says forty-three dollars. The friends add their prices.

"Eight plus ten plus nine plus eleven is thirty-eight," Sara says.

They check the bill. It includes a five-dollar cake, but nobody ordered cake. The waiter removes the mistake.

The friends add a four-dollar tip. Half the group checks the new total: forty-two dollars.

"Can we share it equally?" Jon asks.

Forty-two cannot make four equal shares in whole dollars. Each person pays ten dollars, and Maya and Lee each add one more dollar.

Lee takes a wallet from his bag, and the waiter gives them a correct receipt.

Before they leave, Sara checks the numbers one last time. A simple addition saved them five dollars and found a mistake.
