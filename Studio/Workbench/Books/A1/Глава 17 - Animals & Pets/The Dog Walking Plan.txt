The Dog Walking Plan

Maya wants a dog, but her parents say a pet needs daily care.

They make a one-week plan with their neighbour's dog, Bruno. He is active and friendly. His owner asks Maya to walk him twice a day, fill his water bowl, feed him at six, and use his lead.

On Monday morning, Maya wakes up early and uses Bruno's lead. In the evening, rain is falling, but Bruno still needs a walk.

On Wednesday, Maya wants to meet friends after school. She first goes home to feed Bruno.

During the walk, Bruno stops to look at a horse behind a fence.

By Sunday, she understands that a dog is not only games and soft fur. A dog has a schedule every day.

Her parents ask, "Do you still want a pet?"

"Yes," Maya says, "but not this month. I want to be ready first."

She continues walking Bruno once a week. Learning to care for an animal helps her make a careful choice.
