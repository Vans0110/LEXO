A Voice from Online

Yuki is in Japan, and Ben is in Australia. They are language partners. Today is their first online call.

Ben calls at seven o'clock. A friendly face appears on Yuki's phone.

"Hello! Am I speaking to Yuki?"

"Yes. And you are Ben?"

"That is right. Nice to meet you."

They talk about their countries and cities. Ben speaks slowly. When Yuki does not understand, she asks, "Can you repeat that, please?"

At the end, Ben gives Yuki his email address.

"Can you spell your first name and last name?" Yuki asks.

"WALKER," Ben says.

Yuki writes it down and reads it back. Then she gives Ben her phone number. They check every digit.

The call is only fifteen minutes long, but both students use real English. They plan another call for next Tuesday.
