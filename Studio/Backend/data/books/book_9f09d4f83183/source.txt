The Cooking Problem

Liam wants to cook dinner for his friend Sophie. His list has tomatoes, cheese, pasta, bread, and juice.

At home, Liam cuts vegetables with a knife. He puts some vegetables in a pan, tomato sauce in a pot, and bread in the oven. Then his phone rings.

When he returns, he smells something bad. Bread can burn quickly, and this bread is black.

"Oh no," Liam says. "I burned it."

Sophie arrives and looks at the bread. Then she looks at the vegetables.

"We still have some good food," she says. "We can cook together."

Liam watches the sauce while Sophie makes a salad in a large bowl. They put the pasta on two plates and use forks to eat it.

"Do you like the sauce?" Sophie asks.

"Yes, I do. I don't like the bread."

They laugh and put the black bread in the bin. Dinner is easier with two people.
