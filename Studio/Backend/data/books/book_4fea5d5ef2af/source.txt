The English Teacher's Bag

After class, Mr Brown leaves a black bag under his classroom desk. Five students find it.

"Does this bag belong to Mr Brown?" Eva asks.

"Yes," Tom says. "His name is on the folder."

They open the bag to find a phone number. Inside there are three blue pens, two pencils, a red folder, a ruler, a small dictionary, and a book.

"Teachers carry a lot of things," Eva says.

The dictionary has the school office number on the first page. Tom calls the office.

Mr Brown returns ten minutes later.

"Thank you," he says. "I need that folder for my next lesson."

He checks the bag. Everything is there.

The next day, Mr Brown writes a new sentence on the board: `These students are careful and kind.`

The class knows exactly who he means.
