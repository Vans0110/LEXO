The Lost Wallet

Chris goes to a market with fifty dollars. He buys apples for four dollars, bread for three, and vegetables for eight.

Then he wants coffee. When he reaches into his pocket, his wallet is gone.

Chris walks back past every market table. He checks near the fruit, under a bench, and beside the bread shop.

An older woman holds up a brown wallet.

"Is this yours?"

Chris describes it. "There are thirty-five dollars in cash, two notes, three coins, one bank card, and a family photo inside."

The woman opens it. The amount, card, and photo are correct.

"I found it near the tomato table," she says. "I wanted to return it to its owner."

Chris thanks her and offers to buy her coffee. Two coffees cost six dollars.

After paying, Chris puts the wallet in a zipped pocket because he does not want to lose it again. He came to the market to spend money, but the most valuable thing he found was an honest person.
