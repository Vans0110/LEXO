Our Summer Plan

The Reed family is planning a five-day holiday and journey in August. They want to visit a new place.

Mum wants to visit the beach. Dad prefers the mountains. Their daughter wants a hotel with a swimming pool.

They compare two places. The distance to Blue Bay is two hundred kilometres. The hotel costs one hundred and twenty dollars a night. Hill Town is ninety kilometres away, and a small apartment costs seventy dollars a night.

The weather at Blue Bay is usually hot. Hill Town is cooler and has forest walks.

The family has a budget of five hundred dollars. Five hotel nights at Blue Bay are too expensive. A train carriage is cheaper than plane tickets.

They choose Hill Town for four nights and will stay in the apartment. Each person gets a seat on the train. The apartment has a pool nearby, and there is a lake for swimming.

Their holiday is not exactly Mum's beach plan or Dad's mountain plan. It is a new plan with something for everyone.
