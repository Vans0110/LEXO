# Chapter 8: City & Transport

## Цель главы

Назвать городские места и транспорт, спросить дорогу, понять маршрут.

## Ядро

- Слова: street, road, bridge, corner, bank, station, stop, platform, bus,
  train, metro, taxi, bicycle, map, ticket, straight, turn, cross.
- Данные: адреса, номера маршрутов, остановок и платформ, стоимость.
- Грамматика: `can/can't`, императив, предлоги движения.

## Book 1: Lost in the City

Target: lost, directions, straight, turn left, bank, station, battery.
Recycle: city, street, friend, phone.

Ethan visits a large city for the first time. He wants to meet Daniel at the train station.

His phone map shows the way, but after ten minutes the battery is dead. Ethan is lost. A bus, taxi, and bicycle pass him. He walks down King Street, crosses a small bridge, and turns at the wrong corner.

At last, he sees a police officer.

"Excuse me. Can you help me find Central Station?"

"Yes. Go straight for two blocks. Turn left at the bank, cross River Road, and you will see the station."

Ethan repeats the directions. "Straight, left at the bank, then across River Road."

He follows the route carefully. Soon he sees a large clock above the station door. Daniel is waiting outside.

"You are twenty minutes late," Daniel says.

"My phone is dead, but I can still ask for directions."

Before they leave, Ethan buys a small paper map. It does not need a battery.

## Book 2: The Bus to River Street

Target: route, stop, fare, change, direct, get on, get off.
Recycle: bus, number, ticket, street.

Nora needs to go to fifty-eight River Street. At the bus stop, she sees Route six and Route sixteen on the sign.

"Does Route six go to River Street?" she asks a passenger.

"Yes, but you must change buses at Market Square. Route sixteen is direct."

Route sixteen arrives first. Nora gets on and pays the two-dollar fare. The driver gives her a ticket as the bus turns left.

"Which stop is near number fifty-eight?" Nora asks.

"Get off at River Street Library. It is the sixth stop."

Nora counts the stops. At the fifth, she gets ready. At the sixth, she gets off beside the library.

Number fifty-eight is across the street. The whole trip takes eighteen minutes.

On the way home, Nora takes Route sixteen again. This time, she does not need to ask where to get off.

## Book 3: One Stop Too Far

Target: next, previous, end, return, opposite, wait.
Recycle: metro, stop, map, get off.

Kai takes the metro to a sports centre. He needs Green Park Station, Stop seven.

On the train, Kai reads a message and does not hear the station name. When he looks up, the doors are closing at Stop eight.

"I went one stop too far," he says.

He gets off at the next station and looks at the map. The previous stop was Green Park. The train on the opposite platform returns toward it.

Kai crosses the station, waits three minutes, and gets on the return train. This time, Green Park is the next stop.

Outside, the sports centre is opposite the station. Kai arrives seven minutes late.

After practice, he puts his phone in his pocket on the metro. He watches the station numbers instead.

At the end of the day, the journey home has twelve stops, and Kai gets off at exactly the right one.

## Book 4: Meet Me by the Bridge

Target: bridge, corner, beside, opposite, entrance, distance, block.
Recycle: address, walk, turn, street.

Leah and Tom plan to meet by a bridge, but the city has three bridges.

"I am at a bridge on Lake Road," Tom says on the phone.

"I am on River Street," Leah answers. "What is near you?"

Tom sees a bank on one corner and a hotel opposite it. The bridge is beside the hotel entrance.

"I know that place," Leah says. "Walk two blocks along Lake Road. I am near a bookshop at number thirty."

Tom walks, but Leah is not there. Then he sees another bookshop across the road.

"Which side are you on?"

"The side with the red cafe."

Tom goes straight, then turns left and crosses at the lights. A taxi and two bicycles wait near the bridge. The distance to Leah is only fifty metres. She is waiting beside the cafe, south of the hotel and north of the river.

They write the full meeting place in their phones: `Lake Bridge, hotel side, opposite the bank.` Next time, one bridge will be enough information.

## Book 5: The Smallest Map

Target: map, line, platform, north, south, change, journey.
Recycle: train, metro, station, number.

Ada has a very small city map. It fits inside her ticket holder and shows the full metro route.

She is at South Station and wants to visit North Museum. The map shows two metro lines: blue and yellow.

Ada takes the blue line from Platform two. After four stops, she changes at City Hall. Signs point to the yellow line upstairs.

"Which platform goes north?" she asks a worker.

"Platform five."

The yellow train arrives in two minutes. North Museum is the third stop.

The fare is three dollars, and the journey takes twenty-five minutes. When Ada leaves the station, the museum is on her right.

Her small map has no pictures of restaurants or shops. It only has lines, stations, and numbers. That is enough.

On the return journey, Ada helps another visitor change from the yellow line to the blue one.
