Four Seasons at One Window

Ella takes one photograph from her kitchen window every season. A forest and small hill are visible behind the tree.

In March, spring begins. Small green leaves grow on the tree, and yellow flowers open below it. A river and lake shine behind the garden. Rain falls often.

In July, summer makes the tree full and green. The days are long and warm. Children play outside in the evening.

In October, autumn colours the leaves orange and red. Wind moves them across the street.

In January, winter covers the branches with snow. The days are short, and the window is cold.

Ella puts the four photographs together. The seasons change the tree, so it looks like four different trees.

Her young nephew points to the autumn picture. "Is that today?"

"No. Today is spring," Ella says. "But every season comes back."

They look outside. New leaves are growing again.
