# Chapter 11: Health & Body

## Цель главы

Назвать части тела и простые симптомы, сообщить о самочувствии, понять совет.

## Ядро

- Слова: head, face, eye, ear, nose, mouth, tooth, neck, arm, hand, finger,
  leg, foot, back, stomach, hurt, pain, sick, cold, cough, medicine.
- Данные: температура тела, время приёма, количество таблеток.
- Грамматика: `feel`, `hurt`, `have`, базовое `should`.

## Book 1: A Cold on Monday

Target: cold, cough, nose, throat, temperature, sick, rest.
Recycle: Monday, morning, work, phone.

On Monday morning, Eva wakes up with a sore throat. Her nose is blocked, one ear hurts, and she has a cough. She opens her mouth and sees that her throat is red.

She also has a headache and pain in her neck. She does not take a tablet or other medicine before speaking to a doctor.

She takes her temperature. It is thirty-eight degrees.

Eva calls her manager.

"I feel sick," she says. "I cannot come to work today."

Her manager tells her to rest. Eva makes warm tea and sits on the sofa with a blanket. She drinks water and sleeps for two hours.

She makes a doctor's appointment for the next morning in case she feels worse.

At noon, her friend Kim brings soup and fruit. Kim leaves the bag outside the door because she does not want to catch the cold.

In the evening, Eva's temperature is thirty-seven point five. She still has a cough, but her throat feels better.

She sends a message to the office: `I am resting today. I will call again tomorrow morning.`

Sometimes the best Monday plan is to stay home.

## Book 2: My Foot Hurts

Target: foot, ankle, hurt, pain, walk, bandage, careful.
Recycle: park, shoe, doctor, left/right.

Sam is running in the park when his left foot lands on a small stone. His ankle turns, and he stops.

"My foot hurts," he tells his friend Ana.

Sam can stand and walk, but his leg, foot, and ankle hurt and give him pain. Ana is careful when she helps him sit on a bench and takes off his shoe. The ankle is red and looks larger than the other one.

They go to a nearby clinic. A nurse looks at the foot.

"Can you move your toes?"

"Yes, but my ankle hurts."

The nurse puts a bandage around his foot and ankle. She tells Sam to rest and keep the foot up.

"You should not run for a few days."

Ana calls a taxi because the bus stop is far away.

At home, Sam puts his foot on a pillow. His running shoes wait by the door, but today he is not going anywhere.

## Book 3: The Kind Doctor

Target: appointment, patient, symptom, stomach, examine, better, worse.
Recycle: name, age, form, time.

Mila has a doctor's appointment at ten thirty. In the waiting room, one child holds a tooth, and another patient has a bandage on one finger. At the clinic, Mila fills in a short form with her name, age, address, and phone number.

Dr Shah calls his patient into the room.

"How do you feel?"

"My main symptom is pain. My stomach hurts, and I feel tired."

"Do you also feel dizzy or have a cold, cough, tooth pain, or a problem with your ear, nose, or mouth?"

"No," Mila says.

"When did the pain start?"

"Last night after dinner."

The doctor examines her stomach, face, eyes, arms, hands, and back. He asks what she ate. Mila had fish at a cafe. Her friend ate the same meal and feels fine.

Dr Shah checks Mila's head, face, eyes, and temperature. It is normal.

"Drink water and eat simple food today," he says. "If the pain gets worse, call the clinic."

He writes the advice on a small paper.

That afternoon, Mila rests. By evening, she feels better. She keeps the clinic number near her phone, but she does not need to call.

## Book 4: Tea for a Sick Friend

Target: tablet, medicine, instruction, before, after, twice, dose.
Recycle: tea, water, friend, time.

Leo visits his sick friend Rosa. She also has tooth pain. She has medicine from the pharmacy, but the instructions are small.

"Can you read this?" Rosa asks.

Leo reads the label: `Take one tablet twice a day after food.`

"You take one after breakfast and one after dinner," he explains. "That is the correct dose."

Rosa looks at the clock. It is three in the afternoon. Too much medicine can hurt her stomach, so she follows the dose.

"Should I take one now?"

"Did you take one after breakfast?"

"Yes."

"Then wait until after dinner."

Leo has a small bandage on his hand, but he can still make tea and bring a glass of water. He writes two times on a note: `eight o'clock breakfast — tablet. seven o'clock in the evening dinner — tablet.`

Rosa puts the note beside the medicine.

Before Leo leaves, he checks that she has enough food and water.

The medicine is important, but clear instructions and a caring friend help too.

## Book 5: The Sports Day Headache

Target: headache, dizzy, energy, sit down, shade, enough, decide.
Recycle: hot, water, temperature, sport.

It is sports day at school, and the temperature is thirty-two degrees. Before the race, students turn their heads and necks, move their arms and fingers, and stretch their backs and legs. Ben's face is hot, and his eyes hurt in the bright sun. He is ready for the four-hundred-metre race.

Before the race, he has little energy, gets a headache, and feels a little dizzy. These symptoms worry his teacher.

His teacher asks, "Did you eat breakfast?"

"Only an apple."

"Did you drink enough water?"

Ben looks at his full bottle. "No."

The teacher takes him to the shade. Ben sits down, drinks water slowly, and eats a sandwich.

After twenty minutes, he feels better, but the race is starting.

"You can still run if you want," the teacher says, "but you should listen to your body."

Ben decides not to race. He moves his feet and ankles slowly, stays in the shade, and then helps record the runners' times instead.

He is sad for a moment, but later he understands: sports day is one day. Health is needed every day.
