The Room with Two Doors

Maya visits her cousin's new home. After a long bus ride, she wants to wash her hands.

"Where is the bathroom?" she asks.

"Go down the hall," her cousin says. "There are two doors. The bathroom is on the left."

Maya opens the left door. There is a bed, a wardrobe, and a small desk.

"This is the bedroom," she calls.

"Sorry! The bathroom is on the right."

Maya opens the other door. There is a mirror above the sink, a towel next to the shower, and soap on a small shelf.

Later, her cousin puts a blue sign on the bathroom door. It has no words, only a picture of a shower.

"Now guests can find the right room," he says.

Maya laughs. "Or you can remember which side is left."
