The Sunny Morning

Chapter 1: The New Day

Tom wakes up at seven o'clock in the morning. The sun is bright. He is happy. He goes to the kitchen and sees his cat, Luna. Luna is small and grey.

Good morning, Luna! Tom says.

The cat says, Meow.

Chapter 2: Breakfast

Tom makes breakfast. He eats eggs and toast. He drinks orange juice. Luna eats her cat food. Tom looks out the window. He sees a big garden with red flowers and green trees.

It is a beautiful day, Tom thinks.

Chapter 3: The Park

At ten o'clock in the morning, Tom goes to the park. He wears a blue T-shirt and white shoes. In the park, he sees his friend, Anna.

Hello, Anna! How are you? Tom asks.

I am great, thank you! Anna says.

They walk together. They see a big dog. The dog is brown and very friendly.

Chapter 4: Home

In the afternoon, Tom goes home. He is a little tired but very happy. He reads a book on the sofa. Luna sleeps on his legs.

Goodnight, Luna, Tom whispers.

It is a very good day.
