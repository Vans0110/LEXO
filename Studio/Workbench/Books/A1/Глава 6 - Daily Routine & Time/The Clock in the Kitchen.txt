The Clock in the Kitchen

At night, the clock in Jamie's kitchen is ten minutes slow. Jamie does not know this.

On Tuesday, he looks at the clock. It says eight twenty. His meeting starts at nine, and the office is thirty minutes away.

Jamie leaves at eight twenty-five. At the bus stop, his phone says eight thirty-five.

"Which time is correct?" he asks.

The phone is correct. Jamie takes the next bus, but traffic is heavy. He arrives at nine five and misses the start of the meeting.

That evening, Jamie checks every clock in the apartment. The bedroom clock is five minutes fast. The kitchen clock is ten minutes slow. His phone and computer show the same time.

Jamie never wants to miss another meeting. He fixes both clocks without hurrying. Then he writes tomorrow's meeting time on a note: `ten o'clock — leave at nine twenty`.

The next day, he arrives ten minutes early.
