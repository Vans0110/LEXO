A Birthday for Dad

The date is May fourteenth, and Oliver's father is forty-nine today. The family wants to give him a surprise after work.

Oliver buys a small cake. His sister Mia buys five large candles and four small candles. The large candles mean ten years each.

"Five large candles are fifty," Oliver says. "Dad is forty-nine."

Mia removes one small candle. "Now it is forty-nine."

Their kind aunt, funny uncle, and young cousin arrive early. The cousin is their aunt and uncle's daughter. Dad's wife makes her husband's favourite dinner. Their younger son hides the present under the table. He almost tells Dad about it on the phone, but Mia stops him.

At seven, Dad opens the door. The room is dark.

"Is anyone home?" he asks.

The lights come on. "Happy birthday!"

Dad looks at the cake, the candles, and his family. He remembers every family birthday, but today everyone remembers his.
