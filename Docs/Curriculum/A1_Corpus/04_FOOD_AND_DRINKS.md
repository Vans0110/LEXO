# Chapter 4: Food & Drinks

## Цель главы

Назвать продукты и напитки, выразить предпочтение, купить или заказать еду.

## Ядро

- Слова: bread, rice, pasta, meat, chicken, fish, egg, cheese, fruit,
  vegetables, water, tea, coffee, juice, bottle, bag, box, kilo, menu, bill.
- Предметы: pot, pan, knife, spoon, fork, plate, glass.
- Грамматика: `like/don't like`, `some/any`, простые просьбы.

## Book 1: The Cooking Problem

Target: cook, pan, pot, knife, sauce, salad, burn, together.
Recycle: bread, vegetables, table, some.

Liam wants to cook dinner for his friend Sophie. His list has tomatoes, cheese, pasta, bread, and juice.

At home, Liam cuts vegetables with a knife. He puts some vegetables in a pan, tomato sauce in a pot, and bread in the oven. Then his phone rings.

When he returns, he smells something bad. Bread can burn quickly, and this bread is black.

"Oh no," Liam says. "I burned it."

Sophie arrives and looks at the bread. Then she looks at the vegetables.

"We still have some good food," she says. "We can cook together."

Liam watches the sauce while Sophie makes a salad in a large bowl. They put the pasta on two plates and use forks to eat it.

"Do you like the sauce?" Sophie asks.

"Yes, I do. I don't like the bread."

They laugh and put the black bread in the bin. Dinner is easier with two people.

## Book 2: Breakfast for Two

Target: breakfast, egg, toast, cereal, milk, hungry, enough.
Recycle: cup, plate, spoon, tea, coffee.

Ben and Rosa share a kitchen, but they like different breakfasts.

Ben likes eggs and toast. Rosa likes cereal with milk and fruit.

On Monday morning, there is one egg, two pieces of bread, some fruit, and a little milk in the fridge.

"There is not enough food for both breakfasts," Ben says.

Rosa looks in a cupboard. She finds a packet of oats and two bananas. She cooks the oats in a pot while Ben cooks the egg in a small pan.

"We can make one big bowl of oats," she says.

Ben does not usually like oats, but he is hungry. Rosa adds milk and banana. Ben makes tea and coffee and puts two cups on the table.

They share the bowl and eat toast from one plate.

"Do you like it?" Rosa asks.

Ben takes another spoonful. "Yes. But tomorrow I am buying eggs."

Rosa smiles. "And more milk."

## Book 3: The Last Apple

Target: kilo, price, cost, cheap, expensive, coin, change.
Recycle: apple, fruit, buy, number.

At the market, Nina passes bags of rice and a fish table. One apple is left in a fruit box. She has a bottle of water and reaches for the apple at the same time as an older man.

"You can have it," the man says.

"No, please take it," Nina answers.

The seller smiles. "I have more apples under the table."

She brings out a full bag. The price is three dollars per kilo. The apples do not cost very much. They are not especially cheap or expensive. Nina buys one kilo, and the man buys two apples.

Nina gives the seller a five-dollar note. The seller gives her two dollars in change.

The older man checks his pocket. He has only one large coin.

"How much is one apple?" he asks.

"Fifty cents."

Nina changes his coin for two smaller coins. Everyone gets an apple.

"That last apple was not really the last one," Nina says.

"No," the seller replies, "but it helped three people talk."

## Book 4: Lunch at the Corner Cafe

Target: menu, order, soup, sandwich, bill, waiter, anything else.
Recycle: price, water, juice, like.

Adam visits a small cafe near his school for lunch. He reads the menu by the window. The breakfast menu and an old bill are still on the next table.

Soup is four dollars. A cheese sandwich is five dollars. The menu also has chicken with rice, fish, and a meat sandwich. Juice is two dollars, and coffee is one dollar.

A waiter comes to the table. "Are you ready to order?"

"What would you like?"

"Can I have the vegetable soup and a cheese sandwich, please?"

"Would you like anything to drink?"

"Just water, please."

"Anything else? Perhaps some juice?"

"No, thank you. I do not need anything else."

The waiter brings a bowl, a plate, a spoon, and a glass of water. Adam likes the soup, but the sandwich is very large. At the next table, a family orders fish and rice. Adam eats half his lunch and asks for a small box for the rest.

"Can I have the bill, please?"

The bill is nine dollars. Adam pays ten dollars and leaves one dollar on the table.

At dinner, he eats the other half of the sandwich. One cafe lunch becomes two meals.

## Book 5: A Picnic with No Bread

Target: picnic, basket, bottle, packet, piece, share, forget.
Recycle: food, fruit, cheese, juice, some/any.

Four friends plan a picnic in the park. Each person brings something.

Maya brings a bottle of juice and four cups. Leo brings a bag of apples. Sofia brings cheese, tomatoes, cold pasta salad, two eggs, chicken pieces, meat sandwiches, and a packet of biscuits. Dan brings the picnic basket.

A park cafe has a lunch menu with tea, but the friends do not want another bill. They already have enough food.

They meet after breakfast, sit under a tree, and open the basket. It has plates, forks, a knife, and one kilo of fruit, but there is no bread.

"Who has the bread?" Maya asks.

Everyone looks at Dan.

"I forgot it," he says. "Next time, I will not forget."

They cannot make cheese sandwiches, so Sofia cuts the cheese and tomatoes into small pieces. They add them to the pasta with a simple sauce and share the apples and biscuits.

A family at the next table has extra bread. They offer the friends four pieces. In return, the friends give the family some apples.

The picnic is not the meal they planned, but everyone is hungry and has enough food.
