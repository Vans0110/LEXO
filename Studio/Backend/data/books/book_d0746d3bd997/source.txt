New Apartment

Oliver moves into Apartment eight on the third floor of a building in the city. The apartment is small, but it has everything he needs.

There is a green sofa in the living room. A low table is in front of it. There are two chairs near the window and a lamp next to the sofa.

In the bedroom, there is a bed and a large wardrobe. Oliver puts his clothes in the wardrobe and his books on a shelf.

His friend Mia comes to help. She carries a plant into the living room.

In the kitchen box, she finds a cup, plate, glass, spoon, fork, knife, and kettle. Oliver puts them beside the fridge and keeps his key on the shelf.

"Where do you want this?" she asks.

"Put it on the table by the window, please."

They sit on the floor because the chairs are still in boxes. Oliver looks at the sofa, the lamp, and the books.

"It does not look like a box anymore," Mia says.

Oliver smiles. It looks like a home.
