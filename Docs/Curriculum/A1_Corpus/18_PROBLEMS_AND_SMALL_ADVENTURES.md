# Chapter 18: Problems & Small Adventures

## Цель главы

Рассказать о небольшой проблеме или приключении в ясной последовательности.

## Ядро

- Слова: happen, suddenly, lose, miss, break, fall, wrong, strange, search,
  discover, escape, safe, lucky, finally.
- Грамматика: Past Simple частотных глаголов, последовательность событий.

## Book 1: The Key under the Chair

Target: lose, search, key ring, remember, finally, find.
Recycle: apartment, chair, pocket, floor.

On Friday evening, something strange happened: Amir reached his apartment and could not find his key. He did not want to lose it, so the search began.

First, he checked every pocket. Then he opened his backpack and took out his books, phone, and wallet.

He remembered using the key in the morning. After that, he visited a cafe and the library.

Amir walked back to the cafe. The worker found no key. At the library, he almost fell near a chair, then looked under the study table.

Then a student pointed to a chair. A blue key ring was under one leg.

"Is this yours?"

Amir described the two silver keys and the small number eight on the ring. It was his.

He put the key ring safely in his pocket.

He was lucky and finally returned home two hours late.

The next day, Amir attached the keys to a bright red strap inside his bag. He had a small adventure, but he did not want to repeat it.

## Book 2: The Wrong Shopping Bag

Target: wrong, notice, receipt, exchange, similar, return.
Recycle: shop, clothes, food, number.

Rosa suddenly left a shop with a green paper bag. At home, she opened it and noticed men's shoes in size forty-five. It was the wrong bag, but it looked similar to hers.

She had bought a small blue sweater.

Rosa checked the receipt. Her sweater cost thirty dollars. The shoes cost seventy. The bag was not hers.

She returned to the shop. A man was standing at the counter with another green bag.

"I think we exchanged bags," he said.

His receipt showed the shoes. Rosa's sweater was in his bag.

The assistant checked both receipt numbers. Then they discovered how the exchange happened. Rosa and the man had paid at the same time and put their similar bags beside each other.

They exchanged the bags. Rosa opened hers and found the blue sweater inside. She held the other bag carefully because she did not want to drop it or lose the man's shoes.

The bags looked exactly the same. The man wrote his name on his bag before leaving. Rosa tied a yellow scarf around hers.

They laughed, checked the contents, and left with the right bags.

## Book 3: The Lift Stopped

Target: lift, stop, button, alarm, wait, rescue, safe.
Recycle: floor, building, phone, calm.

Mia and an older neighbour entered the lift on the sixth floor. They pressed the ground-floor button. Between floors, the lift suddenly stopped. The lights stayed on, but the doors did not open.

It made a strange sound, but the lift did not break.

The neighbour looked afraid and tried to open the doors. She wanted to escape, but the lift was between floors.

"Please do not push them," Mia said. "We are safe. Let us press the alarm button."

A voice answered through a small speaker. A worker knew their building and lift number. He told them to wait and not push the doors.

Mia called the neighbour's daughter. Then they sat on the floor and talked.

The rescue took fifteen minutes. Then the lift moved slowly to the fifth floor, and a technician opened the doors. Nobody was hurt.

The worried neighbour said sorry for shouting. Mia smiled and helped her stand up. The accident made Mia late, but she did not miss her appointment.

They used the stairs to go down. On the ground floor, both women stopped for tea. Their short lift journey became a longer story.

## Book 4: The Cake on the Floor

Target: drop, floor, accident, ingredient, replace, quickly.
Recycle: cake, party, time, shop.

An accident happened when Ben carried a birthday cake from the kitchen. His foot hit a chair, so he dropped the cake onto the floor.

The party started in one hour.

For a moment, nobody spoke. Then Ben's sister checked the kitchen.

"We have every ingredient: flour, eggs, sugar, and milk. But a new cake needs more time."

Their mother had another idea. They could quickly replace the cake with small pancakes and put fruit and cream on top. Ben watched the pan so they did not burn.

They quickly replaced one large cake with many small cakes, using every ingredient they had.

They exchanged the old birthday plan for a new one.

Ben cleaned the floor while his sister cooked. Dad went to the shop for candles.

When the guests arrived, there was no large cake. Instead, every guest got a small pancake cake with one candle in the centre.

"This is better," one child said. "Everyone can blow out a candle."

The fall changed the plan, but the small cakes made every guest smile. Ben put a candle on the last one and carried it very carefully.

## Book 5: The Bus at the End of the Road

Target: miss, last, road, walk, dark, lucky, adventure.
Recycle: bus, route, map, weather.

Four friends spent the day near a lake. They planned to take the last bus at eight.

At seven fifty, they were still walking toward the stop. They saw the bus turn onto the main road.

"We missed it," Leo said.

The next town was six kilometres away. It was getting dark, and light rain began.

They checked the map. A small hotel was one kilometre away. The receptionist called a local taxi, but it would take thirty minutes.

They searched the map again and discovered a shorter road near the river, but it was too dark to walk there safely.

They escaped the rain inside the hotel. Leo's umbrella broke in the wind, so the friends waited, drank tea, and exchanged stories about the day.

They were lucky: the taxi arrived at nine. The driver took them to the train station in the next town.

The taxi was a welcome rescue from a long walk on the wrong road.

They missed one train but finally reached home late and safe.

"Was it a bad trip?" Maya asked.

"No," Leo said. "It was a good trip with one extra adventure."
