# Chapter 13: Hobbies & Free Time

## Цель главы

Рассказать о хобби, выразить предпочтение и предложить совместное занятие.

## Ядро

- Слова: hobby, music, song, instrument, game, sport, film, book, draw, paint,
  dance, swim, run, collect, practise, favourite, free time.
- Грамматика: `like/love/enjoy + -ing`, `want to`, предложения.

## Book 1: A Guitar Next Door

Target: guitar, play, practise, song, music, loud, quiet.
Recycle: neighbour, evening, like.

In her free time, Ella likes music. Every evening, she hears a guitar from the apartment next door. A guitar is her favourite instrument. Sometimes the music is beautiful. Sometimes the same three notes play again and again.

One day, Ella meets her new neighbour in the hall. He is carrying a guitar.

"Are you the musician?" she asks.

"I am learning," he says. "My name is Tom."

Ella tells him the music is sometimes loud after ten. Tom is surprised.

"I am sorry. I use headphones for my computer, but a guitar has no headphones."

Tom also has a keyboard. They agree that he will practise either instrument before eight in the evening.

Ella likes singing, so Tom asks her to help with a simple song. On Saturday, they practise together for thirty minutes.

The same three notes sound better with a voice. Ella still enjoys quiet evenings, but now she also enjoys music next door.

## Book 2: The Friday Film

Target: film, cinema, comedy, action, show, ticket, choose.
Recycle: Friday, time, price, friend.

In their free time, five friends want to see a film on Friday. The cinema has an action show at six thirty and a comedy at seven.

Ben loves action films. Maya prefers comedies. The others like both.

"The action film is two hours long," Maya says. "The last bus leaves at nine."

The comedy finishes at eight forty. Tickets for both films cost nine dollars.

The friends collect their cinema tickets and vote. Three choose the comedy, and two choose the action film. Ben is not happy at first.

"We can watch the action film online next week," Maya suggests.

They buy five seats in Row six. During the comedy, Ben laughs louder than everyone else.

After the film, they catch the last bus.

"Was it a good choice?" Maya asks.

Ben smiles. "Yes. But next Friday, I choose."

## Book 3: A Game in the Park

Target: team, ball, score, win, lose, join, player.
Recycle: park, Saturday, number, friend.

Omar walks through the park and sees six people playing football. It is his favourite sport, though he also likes to swim. One team has only two players.

"Do you want to join us?" a woman asks.

Omar likes watching football, but he rarely plays.

"I am not very good."

"That is okay. We are playing for fun."

Omar joins the smaller team. The score is two to one. He misses the ball twice, then passes it to another player. She scores.

Now the score is two to two.

In the final minute, the other team scores again. Omar's team loses three to two.

"Sorry," Omar says.

"Why? You helped us score," his teammate answers.

They plan another game for next Saturday at eleven. Omar writes the time in his phone. He did not win, but he found a new activity and six new friends.

## Book 4: The Small Art Club

Target: draw, paint, picture, colour, brush, club, idea.
Recycle: table, room, meet, Tuesday.

Nora enjoys drawing, but she always draws alone. At the library, she sees a notice for a small art club.

The club meets on Tuesday at six. Nora brings pencils and a notebook.

There are four people around a large table. One paints flowers. Another draws buildings. A young man has no idea what to draw.

"Draw something in this room," Nora suggests.

He draws a cup beside a pile of books. Nora draws the people at the table.

The club leader gives everyone a brush and blue, red, and yellow paint. Music plays, and one member does a small dance. Nora paints a second picture, and they make other colours by mixing them.

At the end, they put the pictures on a wall.

Nora's picture is not perfect, but it shows five people making art together. She writes the next meeting in her calendar. Her hobby is still drawing, but it is no longer always a quiet hobby.

## Book 5: The Hobby Exchange

Target: hobby, teach, learn, collect, dance, chess, exchange.
Recycle: music, game, book, practise.

Four friends decide to exchange hobbies for one afternoon.

Lina teaches chess. Mark likes to collect coins and shows everyone his collection. Sara teaches a simple dance, and Joel brings a keyboard and guitar. Each instrument adds a different sound.

First, Lina explains how the chess pieces move. Mark loses in six minutes, but he wants to win the next game.

Next, Mark shows coins from five countries. Each coin has a different number and picture.

Sara turns on music and teaches three dance steps. Joel finds the steps difficult, but he keeps trying.

Finally, Joel plays a short song on the keyboard and guitar. The others learn four notes. Then they draw and paint a score card for their hobby game.

At the end, each person chooses one new hobby to practise. Lina wants to dance. Mark wants to play music. Sara wants to learn chess. Joel wants to swim with a sports team. They also plan a comedy film at the cinema, and Joel asks to see the coins again.

Sharing a hobby does not make it smaller. It gives it another home.
