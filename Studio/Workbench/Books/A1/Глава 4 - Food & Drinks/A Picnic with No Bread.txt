A Picnic with No Bread

Four friends plan a picnic in the park. Each person brings something.

Maya brings a bottle of juice and four cups. Leo brings a bag of apples. Sofia brings cheese, tomatoes, cold pasta salad, two eggs, chicken pieces, meat sandwiches, and a packet of biscuits. Dan brings the picnic basket.

A park cafe has a lunch menu with tea, but the friends do not want another bill. They already have enough food.

They meet after breakfast, sit under a tree, and open the basket. It has plates, forks, a knife, and one kilo of fruit, but there is no bread.

"Who has the bread?" Maya asks.

Everyone looks at Dan.

"I forgot it," he says. "Next time, I will not forget."

They cannot make cheese sandwiches, so Sofia cuts the cheese and tomatoes into small pieces. They add them to the pasta with a simple sauce and share the apples and biscuits.

A family at the next table has extra bread. They offer the friends four pieces. In return, the friends give the family some apples.

The picnic is not the meal they planned, but everyone is hungry and has enough food.
