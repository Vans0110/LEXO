Meet Me by the Bridge

Leah and Tom plan to meet by a bridge, but the city has three bridges.

"I am at a bridge on Lake Road," Tom says on the phone.

"I am on River Street," Leah answers. "What is near you?"

Tom sees a bank on one corner and a hotel opposite it. The bridge is beside the hotel entrance.

"I know that place," Leah says. "Walk two blocks along Lake Road. I am near a bookshop at number thirty."

Tom walks, but Leah is not there. Then he sees another bookshop across the road.

"Which side are you on?"

"The side with the red cafe."

Tom goes straight, then turns left and crosses at the lights. A taxi and two bicycles wait near the bridge. The distance to Leah is only fifty metres. She is waiting beside the cafe, south of the hotel and north of the river.

They write the full meeting place in their phones: `Lake Bridge, hotel side, opposite the bank.` Next time, one bridge will be enough information.
