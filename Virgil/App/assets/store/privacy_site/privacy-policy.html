Virgil Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

Virgil is an app for reading English stories with audio playback, word translation, and vocabulary cards.

What data we collect

Virgil does not use ads, analytics, Firebase Analytics, or Crashlytics.

The app may store the following data locally on the user's device:

  • app settings;
  • downloaded books;
  • saved words and vocabulary cards;
  • local reading progress and app state.

This data is stored locally on the user's device.

Internet access and book downloads

Virgil uses internet access to download the book catalog and book files from cloud storage. During these downloads, the server may technically receive standard network data, such as IP address and request information.

Feedback

If a user sends feedback through the Feedback button, Virgil opens the user's email client. The user chooses whether to send an email to the support address. Virgil does not send email automatically without user action.

Notifications and audio

Virgil may use a system notification to control audio playback while reading. This is required for the audio player experience.

Sharing data with third parties

We do not sell users' personal data and do not use it for advertising.

Data retention and deletion

Local data can be removed by deleting the app or clearing the app's data on the device. Feedback emails can be deleted on request by contacting support.

Contact
