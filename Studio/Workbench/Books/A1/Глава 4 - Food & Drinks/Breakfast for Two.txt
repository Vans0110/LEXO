Breakfast for Two

Ben and Rosa share a kitchen, but they like different breakfasts.

Ben likes eggs and toast. Rosa likes cereal with milk and fruit.

On Monday morning, there is one egg, two pieces of bread, some fruit, and a little milk in the fridge.

"There is not enough food for both breakfasts," Ben says.

Rosa looks in a cupboard. She finds a packet of oats and two bananas. She cooks the oats in a pot while Ben cooks the egg in a small pan.

"We can make one big bowl of oats," she says.

Ben does not usually like oats, but he is hungry. Rosa adds milk and banana. Ben makes tea and coffee and puts two cups on the table.

They share the bowl and eat toast from one plate.

"Do you like it?" Rosa asks.

Ben takes another spoonful. "Yes. But tomorrow I am buying eggs."

Rosa smiles. "And more milk."
