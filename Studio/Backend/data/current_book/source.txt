The Big Adventure of Sarah and Leo
Chapter 1: A Special Saturday
Today is Saturday. Sarah is at home. She lives in a small house in a big city. Sarah is a teacher. She loves books, coffee, and her dog, Leo. Leo is a big golden retriever. He is very energetic and funny.

It is 9:00 AM. Sarah looks at Leo. Leo has his leash in his mouth.
"Do you want to go out, Leo?" Sarah asks.
Leo wags his tail. He is very excited. Today, they are not going to the small park near the house. Today, they are going to the Blue Mountain.

Chapter 2: The Journey
Sarah puts on her backpack. In her backpack, she has a bottle of water, two apples, a sandwich, and a ball for Leo. She drives her small red car. Leo sits in the back. He looks out the window. He sees trees, other cars, and tall buildings.

After one hour, they arrive at the mountain. The air is fresh and cold. The sky is blue, and there are no clouds.
"Wow," Sarah says. "It is beautiful here."

Chapter 3: The Forest
They start to walk on a long path. There are many tall trees and yellow flowers. Sarah hears the birds. They sing beautiful songs. Leo runs fast. He smells the grass and the stones.

Suddenly, Leo stops. He looks at a big tree. There is a squirrel. The squirrel is small and brown. Leo wants to play, but the squirrel runs up the tree.
"Come here, Leo!" Sarah calls. Leo comes back and sits down. Sarah gives him a small treat. "Good boy," she says.

Chapter 4: The Picnic
At 12:30 PM, Sarah is hungry. She finds a big, flat rock near a small river. The water in the river is very clear. Sarah sits on the rock and opens her backpack. She eats her sandwich and a red apple.

Leo drinks some water from the river. Then, Sarah throws the ball. Leo runs and catches it. One, two, ten times! They play for a long time. Sarah feels very relaxed. There is no noise from the city. Only the river and the wind.

Chapter 5: The Top of the Hill
They walk for thirty more minutes. Finally, they are at the top of the hill. Sarah can see the whole valley. She sees a small village and a blue lake far away. She takes a photo with her phone to show her mother.

"Look, Leo! We are very high up!"
Leo barks. He looks happy. The sun is warm on their faces. Sarah sits and thinks about her week. She is ready for a new Monday now.

Chapter 6: Going Home
The sun starts to go down. The sky is now orange and pink. Sarah and Leo walk back to the car. Leo is very tired. He walks slowly.

When they get to the car, Leo falls asleep immediately. Sarah drives home. She listens to quiet music on the radio. At home, she makes a cup of hot tea. Leo sleeps on his bed.

"That was a great adventure," Sarah whispers. She closes her eyes and dreams of blue mountains and happy dogs.
