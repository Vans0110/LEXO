Snow in the Morning

When Noah wakes up, the street is white. Snow is falling, and it looks soft.

The temperature is five degrees below zero. Water can freeze, and some water on the road has turned to ice.

Noah wears a coat, wool hat, gloves, and boots. Outside, the snow is deep near the garden wall.

His neighbour Mrs Chen is careful because she has two shopping bags. Noah carries one bag and helps her across the icy path.

At school, children make a small snowman. By afternoon, the sun appears and the temperature rises to two degrees above zero.

Snow on the roof begins to melt. Water drops onto the path.

Noah's snowman becomes smaller, but it is still there when he goes home.

"Winter made it this morning," he tells Mrs Chen.

"And the warmer weather will take it away," she says.
