Virgil helps learners read short English stories and build vocabulary step by step.

The app includes simple A1-level stories organized by chapters. You can download books, read with audio playback, slow down or speed up narration, tap words to see translations, and save useful words for review.

Features:
- Short English stories for beginner learners
- Chapter-based library
- Audio playback with speed control
- Tap any word to see its translation
- Save words and review them with cards
- Works without account registration

Virgil is designed for focused reading practice and vocabulary building.
